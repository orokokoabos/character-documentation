# The Albagliff Empire

## Overview

The **Albagliff Empire**, commonly shortened to the **Glyph Empire**, is a territorial empire that originated from an expanding coalition of criminal groups centuries before the main story.

Although its founders were criminals, Glyph is an actual empire with territory, provinces, a civilian population, governmental institutions, and a recognized ruling dynasty. Its origins should not be confused with its present organization: it is not simply a criminal network calling itself an empire.

In the present setting, Glyph is a dictatorship.

## History

### Origins Among Criminal Groups

Glyph emerged from groups involved in piracy, raiding, black markets, and other criminal activities. These groups consolidated into an increasingly powerful organization, gathering more members and extending their influence.

Their expansion involved more than recruiting criminals. They conquered territory and gained control over routes and borders, gradually acquiring the resources and territorial reach of a political power.

As their holdings expanded, they also acquired civilian populations. Farmers, workers, and other ordinary inhabitants lived within the territories they conquered. Consequently, the developing empire was never permanently limited to the criminal groups that founded it.

### The Self-Proclaimed Monarchy

The founding leader proclaimed himself a monarch and required his followers and subjects to address him accordingly. He also claimed descent from an ancient king.

**That claim of ancient royal ancestry was false.** However, it was not meaningfully challenged, and his position as monarch became increasingly accepted.

Over time, the repeated recognition of his authority established his family as a royal dynasty in the eyes of its subjects. Later acceptance of the bloodline did not make the original ancestry claim true; the dynasty's legitimacy developed through the sustained treatment of the family as royalty.

### Territorial Expansion

During its rise, Glyph became an increasingly feared expansionist power. It conquered more land, extended its control over surrounding regions, and threatened existing kingdoms.

Its growing strength also affected relationships between other nations. Governments facing the empire's expansion had to consider whether their existing alliances were worth maintaining against such a dangerous opponent.

One of the kingdoms attacked during this period was **Holy Meekaz**, the predecessor of the Grand Navy. That conflict and its diplomatic consequences are documented in [The Grand Navy — History](./the-grand-navy.md#history).

### Institutionalization

As Glyph consolidated its territory, its original criminal structures gradually became institutions of government.

Protection payments became taxes. Gang territories became provinces. Leaders who had previously operated as criminals increasingly acted as government officials.

This transformation accompanied the growth of a broader society within the empire. Governing conquered territory and ordinary civilian populations required an organization that extended beyond the activities of its founding groups.

The result was a functioning territorial empire whose institutions had developed from criminal origins. Its continued existence no longer depended on every inhabitant being a criminal or every government activity being understood as a criminal enterprise.

Its ruling family's position likewise became established through generations of accepted royal status.

## Present Government

By the main story, the Albagliff Empire is a dictatorship.

Its institutional development and recognized dynasty do not mean that it became a democratic or politically liberal state. They explain how an expanding coalition of criminals became an established empire.

## Historical Relationship With the Grand Navy

Glyph's attack on Holy Meekaz established a lasting hostility between the empire and the institution that later became the Grand Navy.

**To understand that hostility, read [The Grand Navy — History](./the-grand-navy.md).** That section contains the full account of the attack, the collapse of Meekaz's allied support, its inability to retaliate, and the grievance preserved within the modern Navy.

The Grand Navy's present refusal to provide its peacekeeping services to Glyph follows from that history.
