## The Anti-Demi-Human Coalition and Leon's Regime

### Overview

The **Anti-Demi-Human Coalition**, also known as **Leon's Regime**, is a large international political, ideological, and military alliance formed through Leon's efforts to unite groups opposed to demi-humans.

The coalition consists of various nations, political organizations, businesses, military forces, influential individuals, and other groups that share an opposition to the continued existence or integration of demi-humans, although their specific beliefs and motivations differ.

Leon serves as the coalition's principal ideological leader and organizer.

**Francesca Badu (End Game Francesca (around 23 years old)), publicly known as Pheme, serves as its symbolic figurehead and is revered by many of its supporters as humanity's savior.**

Despite her position, Francesca is not primarily responsible for organizing the coalition, directing its political activities, or developing its ideology.

### Primary Objective

The coalition's central objective under Leon's leadership is the **extermination of demi-humans**, which Leon believes is necessary to prevent the eventual extinction of humanity.

Demi-humans carry an incurable disease that can spread to other individuals through proximity. The disease affects demi-humans themselves and can also kill humans who contract it. Infected humans do not transmit the disease further.

No known cure or effective treatment exists.

Leon believes that allowing demi-humans to continue existing will inevitably result in the disease causing humanity's extinction.

From his perspective, exterminating demi-humans would involve enormous loss of life but would prevent an even greater catastrophe.

Consequently, Leon regards the extermination of demi-humans not merely as a desirable political outcome, but as a moral obligation necessary for humanity's survival.

**The coalition's extermination campaign is therefore presented by Leon as an effort to save humanity from extinction.**

However, this is Leon's interpretation of the situation. His belief that extermination is necessary does not establish that the disease will inevitably destroy humanity or that extermination is the only possible response.

### Membership and Ideological Differences

The coalition is not composed entirely of individuals who share Leon's exact beliefs.

Opposition to demi-humans exists along a broad ideological spectrum.

Some members sincerely believe that demi-humans represent an existential threat and support extermination because they consider it necessary for human survival.

Other groups are motivated by hostility or prejudice toward demi-humans and use the disease as justification for their beliefs.

Others may favor separation, expulsion, or restrictive policies rather than extermination, while still opposing demi-human integration.

Political interests, military considerations, and other motivations may also influence individual groups' decisions to cooperate with Leon.

These differences do not prevent the formation of a large coalition, because its members share enough common interests to cooperate against opposing political forces.

However, the existence of different motivations within the coalition does not change Leon's own objective.

**Leon personally seeks the extermination of demi-humans, even though not every individual or organization associated with his coalition necessarily holds precisely the same position.**

### Political and Military Opposition

The coalition exists within an already divided international political environment.

Before its formation, powerful nations, organizations, and individuals on opposing sides of the demi-human conflict had prevented either side from decisively imposing its preferred policies.

The consequences of the G-Rank Free For All significantly disrupt this balance.

Meanwhile, the death of an anti-demi-human G-rank individual associated with the World Government's leadership contributes to a pro-demi-human majority within that institution.

The World Government consequently becomes a central force in organizing international opposition to the anti-demi-human coalition.

The resulting conflict is not simply a confrontation between Leon and the World Government.

It involves numerous nations, factions, organizations, and individuals with differing motivations, political interests, and views regarding demi-humans.

As tensions escalate, the opposing alliances become involved in a worldwide ideological and military conflict.
