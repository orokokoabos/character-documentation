# Southwest Extermination Company

## Overview

The **Southwest Extermination Company** is an organization dedicated to combating monsters in the southwestern continent. It developed from the historical **Covenant**, a joint force formed by indigenous peoples and newcomers from the northeastern continent.

The correct contemporary name is Company, not Coalition.

This entry records the historical background needed to understand Aidos's early campaign. It is not a complete account of every civilization or population on the continent.

## Historical Origins

### Newcomers and Indigenous Peoples

Centuries before the main story, people from the northeastern continent crossed the ocean and began establishing settlements in the central region of the southwestern continent.

Their arrival created disputes and tension with the indigenous peoples already living there. There were conflicts, but this description does not establish a full war between those populations.

Both faced a serious monster problem. Monsters roamed the region, attacked settlements, and killed people from either population.

### The Covenant

Despite their tensions, the two populations agreed to cooperate against the common threat and formed the Covenant.

They shared complementary resources. Indigenous participants contributed knowledge of the land, routes, resources, local animals, and dangers. The northeastern newcomers supplied improved technology, equipment, food, and other material support.

Their cooperation allowed them to fight the monsters together.

### The Commonwealth

The cooperating populations also formed a unified government called the **Commonwealth**.

The Covenant was the monster-fighting military organization associated with that shared political arrangement. The Commonwealth and Covenant should not be treated as interchangeable names for the same function.

### Separation Into Later Communities

Approximately a century later, the Commonwealth split.

The resulting communities included the Kingdom of Tarmi and the indigenous peoples returning to separate political existence. Another group moved toward the coast, encountered Atlantean tribes, and over time formed the Kingdom of Liskara.

This history concerns the central region. Many other civilizations exist elsewhere on the continent and are not described by this outline.

### The Later Company

Across the intervening centuries, the Covenant substantially reduced the monster threat. Monsters continued to exist, but the situation became much more manageable.

By the main story, centuries later, the organization has become known as the Southwest Extermination Company.

## Azerina's Campaign

In the early arcs (like the first few arcs of the entire main series/story), monsters begin rising again under **Azerina**, an intelligent dark-magic dragon whose danger is approximately G-rank level.

Previously separate monsters become coordinated forces. They destroy many settlements and a large part of indigenous territory, then threaten the other kingdoms.

The Company recruits additional personnel and works alongside armies, other hired military organizations, guilds, and adventurers. Human–demi-human prejudice and widespread distrust severely impair that cooperation.

Aidos restores confidence through successful campaigns and eventually coordinates the forces into a decisive night operation. While the participants carry out their assignments against the monsters, he confronts and kills Azerina.

For his role and the detailed dragon fight, see [Aidos's early story](../../other-characters/aidos.md). Those events are recorded there rather than repeated in this organizational history.
