# Tarmi's Guild System

## Overview

The **Kingdom of Tarmi** established a guild system through which adventurers can register guilds, accept assignments, and earn rewards for clearing dungeons, defeating monsters, and protecting civilians.

The organization administering the system registers participating guilds and manages this paid work. Adventurers travel from around the world to participate, form their own guilds, or join existing ones.

## Area of Operation

The system operates on the southwestern continent, primarily around the Kingdom of Tarmi.

Its operating territory also includes indigenous lands where permission has been granted and extends into the high mountain areas it has claimed. Its activities do not automatically give it authority over the entire continent or every indigenous territory.

## Assignments and Rewards

Participating adventurers and guilds undertake work such as:

- Clearing dungeons.
- Hunting dangerous monsters.
- Protecting civilians and settlements.
- Addressing other local threats covered by available assignments.

They receive payment or other rewards for completing that work. The system provides an organized way to connect people who need these services with adventurers willing to perform them.

## International Recognition and Local Scope

Tarmi's guild system has become internationally recognizable. Its reputation attracts foreign adventurers, and participation has developed a cultural appeal beyond its immediate operating territory.

However, international recognition does not make it a global governing or military power. It is a regional organization administering adventuring and monster-slaying work, broadly comparable to a company that registers participants and organizes paid assignments.

It does not possess authority comparable to the Grand Navy or the World Government. Participation is voluntary, and the wider world does not operate under its rules.

The guild system is important to the communities it serves, but it is only one organization within a much larger setting. The series as a whole does not revolve around guild membership, dungeon clearing, or advancement through its ranks.

## Power Ranking

The Guild System is most famous for inventing the [Power Ranking/Rating System](../../power-ranks.md) that is recognized world wide.
