# Foxden Co.

Foxden is a private military and security company (PMSC) that provides combat and/or security services or transport services. There are about ~100 members only.
It was founded by [Captain Edward](../../other-characters/captain-edward.md), following the events of [Edward's Arc](../../arcs/edwards-arc.md). A few dozen of the members are Ex Grand Navy deserters, and the rest are hired along the way. They have participated in a few other arcs in the story, but their most notable part in the main series' story is their contribution in the world war against Leon's Regime (The Anti Demi Humans), during this war they played a major role in preventing the regime from creating a bio weapon that could wipe out cities of people, more information: [Blue Print Persuit](../../arcs/leon-blueprint-pursuit-and-shipwreck.md).
