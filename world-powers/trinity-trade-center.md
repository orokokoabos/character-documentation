# Trinity Trade Center

## Overview

The **Trinity Trade Center**, usually abbreviated **TTC**, is a large trading city in the central region of the southwestern continent.

It functions as a trading center between the Kingdom of Tarmi, the Kingdom of Liskara, and the southwestern indigenous peoples. Those communities occupy the surrounding region, with TTC serving as their central meeting place for trade.

This description does not assign ownership of the city or establish political arrangements that have not yet been documented.

## Aidos's Campaign and the Memorial Grounds

Following Aidos's defeat of Azerina during the early monster crisis, people from the neighboring communities gather at TTC for a victory ceremony.

A large graveyard is established outside the city for the recovered dead. Aidos participates in building the graves alongside thousands of other workers.

His campaign, the ceremony, and the burial effort are documented in [Aidos's entry](../other-characters/aidos.md). The older regional history is explained under the [Southwest Extermination Company](./organizations-and-entities/south-west-extermination-co.md).

## The Later TTC Arc

TTC is also the location of the [TTC arc](../arcs/TTC.md), at whose end Francesca and Salore first fight, but the TTC arc takes place many arcs after Aidos's arc.
