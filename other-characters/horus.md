# Fleet Admiral Horus

## Overview

**Horus** is a G-rank combatant and the second Fleet Admiral in the [Grand Navy's](../world-powers/the-grand-navy.md) history. The first held the position during a war approximately a century earlier.

During the Navy's crisis in the war against Glyph, the Board of Military Chiefs appoints him to unite the four Admirals and their forces under one commander.

## Motives and Historical Connection

Horus is a distant descendant of the king of Holy Meekaz assassinated during Glyph's expansion. He is deeply invested in the unresolved historical grievance and wants Glyph to answer for what it did.

He also genuinely wants to protect Glyph's civilians and liberate them from the dictatorship. His commitment to civilian welfare coexists with his personal and institutional grievance.

## Role in the War

A trusted Rear Admiral who serves as a close subordinate is secretly a Glyph spy. The spy attempts to poison Horus while he sleeps and destroys their ship in a suicidal attack. Horus survives and reaches Glyph's icy outer territory alone.

He later participates in the G-Rank Free For All, fights alongside Baku, and joins the temporary alliance against Salore. He and Aidos land the finishing blows on Salore. Horus subsequently fights Francesca and is killed.

The detailed sequence belongs in [Glyph vs the Grand Navy](../arcs/glyph-vs-navy.md).
