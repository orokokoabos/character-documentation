# Hideki

## Overview

**Hideki** is the world's most-wanted criminal in the contemporary main story and a G-rank combatant. He leads a crew of approximately a hundred people, including several of the world's hundred most-wanted criminals.

His principal magic is light magic. In the [G-Rank Free For All](../arcs/glyph-vs-navy.md#the-g-rank-free-for-all), he can transform into light and travel at massively faster-than-light speed.

## Character and Treatment of His Crew

Hideki treats his crew as family. He regards its members as brothers, sisters, and people for whom he feels a parental responsibility. Their relationship is not simply an arrangement between criminals who happen to share a ship.

His later pursuit of Francesca and Salore follows their betrayal of that trust and the murder of the people he considers his family.

## Activities and Moral Outlook

Hideki and his crew attack corrupt politicians, tyrants, and governments. He believes the world would be better without the powerful people he considers responsible for its suffering and corruption.

This does not amount to a detailed political doctrine or an elaborate program for restructuring society. His outlook is comparatively direct: remove the people he regards as doing harm.

However, he is not an exclusively benevolent outlaw. His crew also commits robberies and other crimes. He has killed good people, whether unintentionally or because he considers their deaths necessary to reach a greater objective—for example, when they stand between him and a ruler he wants to eliminate.

He is willing to commit immoral acts in pursuit of what he considers a better outcome. His intentions do not make every accusation against him fabricated or every victim deserving.

At the same time, he does not simply enjoy evil or disregard all innocent lives. His willingness to stop a fight that endangers civilians demonstrates a real limit on what he wants to do, even though his conduct elsewhere can violate ordinary moral standards.

## Enemies and Wanted Status

Hideki has killed national leaders, tyrants, and politicians. His activities have brought him into prolonged conflict with the Grand Navy, the Assassin Syndicate, and the World Government.

He considers much of the World Government's leadership and senior administration corrupt. That assessment is his own belief, not a statement that every individual within the institution has been established as corrupt.

He and his crew have fought those institutions for a long time. By this period, they have enemies across many competing powers rather than a stable alignment with one of the major opposing institutions.

## Encounter With Aidos

[Aidos](./aidos.md) encounters Hideki in a city and attempts to bring him to justice.

They begin fighting, but the confrontation damages property and threatens nearby civilians. Aidos decides that they cannot continue fighting there.

Hideki also stops. He does not want to keep endangering random innocent people merely to continue the confrontation.

The encounter ends without a decisive winner. This is an interrupted fight, not proof that either combatant has exhausted every means of defeating the other. They separate, and Hideki returns to his crew and leaves.

## Operations Around Glyph

Before meeting Francesca and Salore, Hideki has already spent a substantial period moving among the Glyph Empire and surrounding nations.

His objective is to assassinate Glyph's dictator. The national hero team is a major obstacle: its members are sufficiently powerful that confronting them would be extremely difficult even with his crew.

Francesca and Salore meet the crew outside Glyph and subsequently travel around the region with them. Hideki's existing campaign explains why his crew is operating there before the later pursuit of the pair.

## Francesca, Salore, and the Crew's Destruction

Francesca and Salore join the crew and travel with it for roughly half a year. The crew accepts them as family.

They later slaughter its members while Hideki is away on a separate mission, seeking the reputational benefit of killing highly wanted criminals.

Hideki returns to discover the massacre and pursues both of them. The pursuit eventually brings him to the G-Rank Free For All, where he proposes temporarily uniting against Salore before Francesca arrives.

Hideki ultimately dies attempting to flee Francesca in his light form.

The detailed massacre, pursuit, temporary alliance, and death remain in [Glyph vs the Grand Navy](../arcs/glyph-vs-navy.md), rather than being repeated here.
