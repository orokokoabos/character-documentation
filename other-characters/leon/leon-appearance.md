# Leon — Appearance

## Overview

Leon's physical appearance undergoes a noticeable transformation throughout the story.

When first introduced, he appears physically neglected, exhausted, and emotionally withdrawn. His appearance reflects a prolonged period of poor self-care and psychological distress.

As he begins organizing the anti-demi-human movement and eventually becomes its leader, his appearance changes considerably.

His later appearance is much more refined, composed, and formal. This becomes his primary and most recognizable character design throughout the majority of his appearances in the story.

## Initial Appearance — Before the Rise of His Movement

When Leon is first introduced, he is a white male with long, messy blonde hair.

His hair is poorly maintained, with several loose strands and bangs falling across his face.

Some individual strands extend over his eyes, partially obscuring them. However, his eyes remain visible through the hair. His bangs do not form a completely opaque covering over his eyes.

He has very pronounced dark circles beneath his eyes, giving him a visibly exhausted appearance.

His face is relatively thin, with noticeably hollow cheeks resulting from inadequate eating and poor self-care.

His overall physique is skinny, without much apparent physical fullness.

Leon also habitually slouches.

His default facial expression is largely blank and emotionless.

## Later Appearance — Leader of the Anti-Demi-Human Movement

As Leon's movement develops, his appearance changes substantially.

### Hair

Leon's hair remains blonde but is cut considerably shorter.

It is no longer excessively long, unkempt, or hanging across his face.

Instead, his hair is neatly maintained, with a middle part that leaves his forehead visible.

His hair has a somewhat wavy texture and naturally flows backward and toward the sides of his head.

The overall silhouette has some resemblance to a short, refined wolf cut, although it is not intended to be an exact example of that hairstyle.

### Eyes and Facial Expression

Leon's dark circles remain visible, although they are considerably less pronounced than during his initial appearance.

His gaze becomes much sharper and more piercing.

The shape and overall impression of his eyes combine characteristics commonly associated with fox eyes and hunter eyes.

They possess some of the narrow, sly-looking quality associated with fox eyes, while also having the focused, piercing appearance associated with hunter eyes.

This produces an expression that can appear slightly suspicious, calculating, or as though Leon is planning something.

However, this is primarily a description of his natural facial appearance and default expression. It does not necessarily indicate that Leon is intentionally acting sly or deceptive.

His usual expression is composed, with a subtle sly-looking quality and an intense gaze.

### Face and Physique

Leon gradually gains weight compared to his initial appearance.

He remains relatively lean but no longer looks malnourished or excessively skinny.

His cheeks are no longer hollow, and his face appears healthier and fuller.

He has a conventionally attractive face, with balanced features and a defined jawline.

Leon is canonically considered good-looking, although his facial design is not intended to be unusually elaborate or visually extreme.

### Posture

Leon no longer habitually slouches.

### Clothing and Uniform

In his later appearance, Leon typically wears an entirely black formal uniform.

The uniform has a somewhat suit-like appearance but is not a conventional business suit.

In particular, it does not include a necktie.

The overall impression is that of formal military or command-oriented clothing rather than ordinary civilian business attire.

He also wears a completely black captain-style hat.

The hat resembles the general shape of a traditional captain's or officer's peaked cap.
