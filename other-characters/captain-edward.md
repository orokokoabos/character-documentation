# Captain Edward Baker

Edward was a Captain in the [Grand Navy](../world-powers/the-grand-navy.md) before he deserted and fled to the Kingdom of Tarmi. 

In the Kingdom of Tarmi he founded the private military company Foxden: [Foxden Co.](../world-powers/organizations-and-entities/foxden.md).

Edward is a blonde adult male with green eyes, he's 25 years old at the time of his arc. 

Edward has his own arc, Francesca is not a main character or side character in the arc, but a very small portion/event in the arc featured Francesca: [Edward's Arc](../arcs/edwards-arc.md). Most of Edward's character and behavior you can see from how he acts in his arc.

## Personality

Edward is a compassionate, dependable captain who develops close bonds with the people under his command. He is patient with his new crew as they adjust to losing their former captain and works to earn their trust.

He regards his crew as family and refuses to abandon them when they are captured. Although an experienced Navy officer, his loyalty ultimately rests with his people and the principles he believes the Navy should uphold. When its leadership prevents their rescue, he is willing to sacrifice his career and become a wanted deserter to bring them home.

Edward leads through personal trust and shared conviction, inspiring others to stand alongside him even when doing so means defying the institution they served.
