# Demon King Orca

The contemporary demon king of the series. When we say Demon King in this repo, we typically refer to Orca. If we say "Original Demon King", we typically refer to Demon King Malu.

Orca is not evil, and is what you would call a "liberal". He is kind person, but he struggles to control the demon nation.

There has been dozens of Demon Kings throughout history, he is the current demon king in this series, and he's only about 80 years old (young for a demon). He tries his best to improve but he is just not very good at his job, and very clumsy. 

He is about at level with an S rank.
