# Baku

## Overview

**Baku** is a member of the [World Government's](../world-powers/organizations-and-entities/world-government.md) governing board and its only G-rank member during the Navy–Glyph war.

He is strongly anti-demi-human. The board contains both pro-demi-human and anti-demi-human leaders, making his position politically consequential.

## Search for Horus

After Fleet Admiral Horus's ship is destroyed, the World Government does not know the attack's true circumstances. It fears that an enemy capable of destroying a G-rank commander's ship could annihilate a conventional rescue force.

Baku volunteers to investigate and search for Horus himself. This mission brings him to Glyph's outer territories and the G-Rank Free For All.

## The Free-for-All

Baku fights alongside Horus. Aidos, despite ordinarily supporting the Navy and World Government in the wider war, targets Baku for arrest because of their conflict over demi-human suffering.

After Salore's death and Francesca's arrival, Baku participates in the coalition against her. He becomes her last surviving opponent.

Francesca kills him with the unstoppable-projectile sniper rifle from Salore's bag. Her earlier recording of “Checkmate” plays from the delayed-voice megaphone at the location and moment of his death.

## Political Consequences

Baku's death removes an anti-demi-human leader and leaves a pro-demi-human majority on the World Government's board.

This change helps explain its later official pro-demi-human stance and mobilization against Leon's regime. It does not mean Francesca killed him to produce that political outcome.
