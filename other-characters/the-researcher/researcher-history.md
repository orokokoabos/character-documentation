# The Researcher — History

## Overview

The Researcher's history originates within the first human civilization of the original cosmos, long before the establishment of the realities in which the franchise's main narrative takes place.

Originally a human scientist, she survived the near-total destruction of her civilization and continued pursuing technological advancement over immense periods of time.

Her development ultimately culminated in a transformation that fundamentally altered her relationship with reality, causality, existence, and creation itself.

Although she eventually attained absolute sovereignty, her history as a human being remains an established part of her existence.

## The Original Earth

The Researcher was born on Earth within the original human civilization.

This civilization had reached an extraordinarily advanced technological stage, possessing capabilities roughly comparable to a hypothetical Type IV civilization on the Kardashev scale.

Humanity had developed technologies capable of preventing biological aging, effectively granting individuals indefinite lifespans.

However, this did not make humans invulnerable. They could still be killed through external means.

Despite its technological achievements, humanity remained cautious of extraterrestrial threats and deliberately attempted to conceal its existence from potentially hostile entities.

## The Destruction of Humanity

At an unspecified point in its history, the original human civilization inadvertently revealed its presence to extremely powerful extraterrestrial entities.

These entities possessed capabilities sufficient to devastate humanity despite its extraordinary technological development.

The resulting catastrophe led to the destruction of almost the entire human civilization.

Only a few hundred individuals survived, including The Researcher.

The survivors were forced into prolonged concealment to avoid complete extinction.

## The Remaining Survivors

Following the destruction of their civilization, the surviving humans continued advancing their technology while remaining hidden from the entities responsible for the catastrophe.

Their concealment and research continued over periods potentially spanning millions or billions of years.

During this time, their technological capabilities increased considerably, eventually reaching levels far beyond those possessed by their original civilization.

Nevertheless, the survivors remained vulnerable to the entities pursuing them.

Over time, the surviving population gradually diminished as individuals were discovered and killed.

Eventually, The Researcher became the sole surviving member of the original human civilization.

## The Last Human

Following the deaths of the remaining survivors, The Researcher continued her scientific development independently.

Without the support of her former civilization, she pursued increasingly advanced technologies capable of manipulating progressively more fundamental aspects of reality.

Her research eventually surpassed conventional physical and cosmological limitations.

The distinctions between technological engineering, reality manipulation, and the alteration of fundamental metaphysical principles became increasingly irrelevant as her capabilities advanced.

Despite these developments, her identity and historical origin remained those of a human scientist from Earth.

## The Time Guardians

At an unspecified stage of her technological development, The Researcher created the Time Guardians.

The Time Guardians function as an autonomous security system responsible for protecting temporal structures from unauthorized interference.

When an entity attempts to interfere with time, the Guardians automatically possess whatever means are necessary to eliminate the source of the violation.

Their capabilities are not restricted to a predetermined collection of abilities. Instead, the required means of enforcement are established automatically according to the nature of the threat.

The extraterrestrial entities responsible for humanity's destruction eventually attempted to cross timelines.

This action triggered the Time Guardians, resulting in the entities' elimination.

Their destruction was not the result of a conventional confrontation with The Researcher. They inadvertently violated the temporal restrictions enforced by a system she had created.

## The Final Ascension

The Researcher's technological advancement eventually culminated in her attainment of an absolute state.

At this stage, her existence ceased to depend upon the physical, temporal, causal, and metaphysical conditions under which she had originally developed.

She gained the capacity to restructure the foundational principles governing creation itself.

Through this authority, she established herself as the ultimate ontological source of all creation belonging to the franchise.

This transformation also altered the relationship between her existence and her original history.

Although The Researcher was historically born within a universe that preceded her, she subsequently restructured the foundations of creation such that even her original cosmos ultimately derives its existence from her.

Her human origin remains historically true. However, it no longer determines, sustains, or limits her completed state.

## Current Existence

Following her ascension, The Researcher possesses complete awareness of past, present, and future without experiencing them as successive events.

Her original history, including humanity's destruction, the deaths of the surviving population, and her own technological development, is comprehended in its entirety.

She no longer depends upon the technological mechanisms that originally enabled her transformation.

The Researcher subsequently established numerous realities, including the reality containing the franchise's main narrative.

The Earth inhabited by the main cast is not the original Earth.

Its inhabitants developed independently under environmental and evolutionary conditions that happened to produce organisms remarkably similar to those of the original human civilization.

The Researcher possesses no particular interest in intervening in the main narrative, despite retaining the unrestricted capacity to do so.

Her historical development has concluded, and her absolute state is complete.
