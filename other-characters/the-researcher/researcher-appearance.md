# The Researcher — Appearance

## Overview

The Researcher most commonly appears as a human woman with a composed, elegant, and slightly severe appearance.

Although she is capable of manifesting herself in different forms, her default appearance resembles that of a refined and highly intelligent researcher rather than a visibly supernatural or heavily ornamented divine being.

Her design combines the visual impression of a scientist with a cold, graceful, and somewhat intimidating presence.

## Physical Appearance

The Researcher has long black hair and striking purple eyes.

Her complexion is pale, contributing to her calm and slightly distant appearance.

Her facial features are delicate and refined, but her expression often gives the impression of restraint, intelligence, and quiet authority rather than warmth or emotional openness.

She is generally presented as attractive in a sharp, elegant, and mature way rather than in an overtly flashy or exaggerated manner.

## Hair and Eyes

The Researcher's hair is black, long, and slightly voluminous, often appearing soft but dramatic in silhouette.

It may frame her face loosely, with strands falling naturally around her forehead and cheeks.

Her eyes are purple and are among her most visually distinctive features.

They contribute significantly to her presence, often making her seem observant, unreadable, and difficult to approach.

## Clothing

The Researcher's outfit is centered around a **lab coat**, reflecting her origin as a scientist.

Her clothing typically presents a clean and professional silhouette while still appearing stylish and refined.

Beneath the lab coat, she wears relatively simple, fitted clothing in dark or neutral colors.

Her overall outfit may include a short black skirt or similarly streamlined lower garment, along with black stockings or tights.

The visual balance of the design is important: she should look like a researcher first, while still retaining an elegant and memorable appearance.

Simple black shoes, closer in style to practical dress shoes or oxford-style shoes.
