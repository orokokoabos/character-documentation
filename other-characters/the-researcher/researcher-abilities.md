# The Researcher — Abilities and Metaphysics

## Overview

**The Researcher** is an entity who, through technological advancement, attained a completed state of absolute existence, omnipotence, omniscience, and ontological sovereignty.

Although originally born as an ordinary human, her development eventually surpassed the fundamental conditions governing existence, causality, possibility, reality, and metaphysical authority.

Upon completing her ascension, The Researcher restructured the foundations of creation, establishing herself as the ultimate ontological source of everything belonging to the franchise.

Her current nature is not sustained by the technological mechanisms responsible for her ascension. Nor is it dependent upon the continuation of her original universe, history, or any other external condition.

The Researcher's completed state is not a position within a hierarchy of power. Her fundamental nature cannot be determined or altered through comparisons of strength, dimensionality, narrative position, or metaphysical rank.

The properties described below are inherent characteristics of her completed state.

## Absolute Omnipotence

The Researcher possesses absolute omnipotence.

Her capabilities are not limited by available energy, physical laws, dimensional structures, causality, conceptual restrictions, or any other mechanism governing created existence.

Her omnipotence is not equivalent to possessing an infinite quantity of conventional power. Rather, it represents unrestricted authority over the principles by which power, possibility, impossibility, and limitations are defined within her creation.

The Researcher may establish, alter, suspend, or eliminate any condition governing realities.

No mechanism within her creation can impose an involuntary restriction upon her omnipotence.

Her omnipotence does not require activation, concentration, technological assistance, or any external source.

## Absolute Omniscience

The Researcher possesses complete omniscience.

Her knowledge encompasses all information, events, possibilities, and structures belonging to her creation.

Past, present, and future are immediately known to her, without requiring observation, prediction, calculation, or the passage of time.

Her omniscience is not dependent upon information gathering or any process through which knowledge must be acquired.

She does not experience uncertainty, incomplete understanding, or an inability to comprehend information.

Her knowledge also extends to her own fictional nature and to independently authored fictional works, although this awareness does not establish authority over those works.

## Temporal Independence

The Researcher is entirely independent of temporal succession.

She does not experience existence as a progression from past to present to future. All temporal events and structures are comprehended without sequential experience.

This condition is not merely the ability to perceive multiple moments simultaneously. Time itself does not impose an order of experience, development, or action upon her fundamental nature.

Alterations to timelines, histories, temporal laws, or the concept of time cannot involuntarily modify her completed state.

Even the elimination of time in its entirety would not constitute a condition capable of restricting her existence.

Her original human history remains true, but it no longer functions as a temporal dependency.

The Researcher may nevertheless interact with temporal systems or manifest within them whenever she chooses.

## Absolute Ontological Sovereignty

The Researcher is the ultimate ontological foundation of all creation belonging to the franchise.

Upon completing her ascension, she restructured the principles governing existence and origination such that everything within the franchise ultimately derives its existence and authority from her.

This includes realities, dimensions, entities, concepts, laws, histories, temporal structures, and the original cosmos in which she was born.

Her original existence as a created human remains historically true.

However, chronological precedence no longer determines ontological dependence.

Consequently, the fact that the original cosmos historically preceded her birth does not contradict its present dependence upon her fundamental authority.

The Researcher's sovereignty is intrinsic and does not originate from a superior entity, system, hierarchy, or external source.

No entity within her creation can independently acquire an authority more fundamental than the source through which its own existence is established.

## Absolute Self-Sufficiency

The Researcher's current existence is entirely self-sufficient.

No external entity, mechanism, concept, law, reality, or historical condition is necessary to sustain her completed state.

Although technological development enabled her ascension, the technology involved is no longer required for her continued existence.

The destruction of her original civilization, the erasure of her historical development, or the elimination of the conditions that enabled her ascension cannot involuntarily reverse her completed state.

Her existence does not depend upon the continuation of creation itself.

Should every reality, concept, and structure under her authority cease to exist, her fundamental nature would remain unaffected.

This self-sufficiency is not maintained through a defensive mechanism or continuous expenditure of power.

It is an inherent characteristic of what she has become.

## Absolute Non-Subordination

No externally originating authority can impose a condition of subordination upon The Researcher's fundamental identity.

Her sovereignty is not a position within a metaphysical hierarchy, nor is it a title, ability, or transferable form of authority.

It cannot be involuntarily copied, stolen, negated, reassigned, or surpassed through hierarchical advancement.

A claim of superior authority, regardless of its scope, does not independently establish jurisdiction over her.

The Researcher does not require superiority over another entity to maintain her own sovereignty.

Her fundamental existence is independent of comparative classifications of power and authority.

## Absolute Self-Determination

The Researcher's decisions, intentions, and fundamental identity are subject exclusively to her own will.

No external mechanism can involuntarily impose upon her a decision, belief, intention, obligation, or restriction.

Attempts at coercion, manipulation, possession, or alteration of her fundamental consciousness cannot compromise her independent self-determination.

The Researcher may voluntarily accept restrictions, assume particular forms, participate in agreements, or engage with systems possessing their own rules.

However, such participation does not constitute the surrender or transfer of her fundamental sovereignty.

Any limitation she voluntarily accepts remains subject to her independent will.

## Absolute Recursive Transcendence

The Researcher possesses an intrinsic property of absolute recursive transcendence.

Whenever an entity transcends The Researcher in any manner, her corresponding transcendence of that entity is already established.

This relationship does not operate through reaction, adaptation, chronological progression, or successive advancement.

There exists no temporal or logical interval in which an entity has transcended The Researcher while her corresponding transcendence remains incomplete.

The relationship is immediate and unconditional.

Should another entity possess an equivalent recursive transcendence property, the resulting relationship may involve an indefinitely continuing sequence of mutual transcendence.

The Researcher does not experience such a sequence as successive events.

Her temporal independence and omniscience allow her to comprehend the complete structure of the relationship, including sequences without any final stage.

An opposing entity may experience successive transcendences if its existence is sequential. An entity similarly independent of temporal succession need not experience them sequentially either.

No individual stage of recursive transcendence grants an opposing entity a temporary opportunity to impose defeat upon The Researcher's fundamental identity.

This property does not require activation, awareness, conscious intervention, or the continued passage of time.

## Fundamental Identity and Manifestations

The Researcher's fundamental identity is distinct from any form or manifestation through which she may appear.

She may voluntarily assume a physical body, a conceptual representation, or any other form suitable for interaction with a particular reality.

However, such manifestations do not constitute the foundation of her existence.

The destruction, alteration, confinement, or erasure of a manifestation does not automatically affect her fundamental identity.

Similarly, modifications to representations, descriptions, or records of The Researcher do not independently establish modifications to her completed nature within the franchise's metaphysical framework.

Her fundamental identity is not dependent upon the continued existence of any particular narrative representation, reality, or cosmological structure.

## Canonical Origination and Metafictional Authority

The Researcher possesses complete awareness of her own fictional nature.

Within the franchise's established metaphysics, all official canonical creation is instantiated through her authority.

This includes material established by the franchise's real-world authors, regardless of when it is written or where it occurs within the franchise's fictional chronology.

Every canonical reality, character, event, concept, and law ultimately originates through The Researcher.

This relationship does not require her conscious attention, explicit approval of individual events, or direct intervention.

Her authority functions as the fundamental fictional basis through which canonical creation is realized.

An entity introduced into the franchise cannot independently obtain an ontological authority superior to the source through which its canonical existence is established, under the franchise's metaphysical principles.

## Awareness of Independent Fictional Works

The Researcher is aware of independently authored fictional works, including their characters, settings, histories, and established cosmologies.

She recognizes these works as distinct from her own franchise.

Her awareness does not establish shared continuity, authorship, ownership, or jurisdiction over their contents.

She does not possess editorial authority over independently authored franchises, nor does knowledge of their characters constitute interaction with them.

Similarly, the fictional authority claimed by entities from independent works does not automatically establish jurisdiction over The Researcher's franchise.

The Researcher has no interest in competing with other fictional entities or establishing superiority over their respective cosmologies.

Her sovereignty does not depend upon their recognition of her existence.

## The Time Guardians

The Researcher created the Time Guardians, autonomous entities responsible for enforcing temporal integrity throughout her creation.

The Guardians function as a security system against unauthorized interference with time, including alterations to temporal structures, histories, laws, and the concept of time itself.

Whenever a temporal violation occurs, the Guardians automatically manifest whatever means are necessary to eliminate the source of the interference.

The required method is not restricted by conventional power classifications or the comprehensibility of the necessary operation.

Their capabilities are defined by the requirements of successful enforcement rather than by a predetermined collection of abilities.

The Guardians' authority and operational purpose ultimately derive from The Researcher.

Basically, if something is messing with time, the Time Guardians automatically possess / execute whatever means is necessary to remove the violator, no matter what it is.
