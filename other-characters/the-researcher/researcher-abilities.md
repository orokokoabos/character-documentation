# The Researcher — Abilities and Metaphysics

## Overview

**The Researcher** began as a genuinely ordinary, contingent human in her original cosmos. Through real research and technology, she genuinely ascended into a being capable of rewriting reality at its most fundamental level. After ascending, she rewrote existence itself so that her **completed fundamental identity** had always been necessary, ungrounded, omnipotent, omniscient, and sovereign.

Her human childhood, limited knowledge, technological work, and ascension were actual events in the **original sequence of events**. They were not a disguise, a voluntary manifestation of a secretly preexisting deity, or merely the discovery of powers she already possessed. Ascension was an actual transformation of that originally human person.

**The retroactive rewrite came afterward.** It did not merely change records or give her a past appearance of divinity. It established necessary existence as an intrinsic property of her completed identity and rewrote the ontological and modal conditions of her creation accordingly. In the resulting existence, her completed identity is necessary at every time and across its possibilities, without depending on her human origin or on the event that originally enabled the rewrite.

The story thus retains both facts at distinct levels: she truly became what she was not in the original sequence, and, following her completed rewrite, what she became is not contingent on having become it. Her original human history remains canonically real as the history of how the rewrite was achieved, but it is not a vulnerability or an ongoing condition of her completed existence.

Her completed state is not a position within a hierarchy of power. Her completed fundamental nature cannot be determined or altered through comparisons of strength, dimensionality, narrative position, or metaphysical rank.

The properties described below are intrinsic to her **completed, retroactively established identity**, not powers secretly present in her original human condition.

## Retroactive Ontological Rewriting

Two descriptions of the Researcher's origin must not be conflated:

1. **Original sequence:** An ordinary human existed contingently, developed technology, and truly ascended. At this stage, she was not yet the necessarily existent, inviolable Researcher described by her completed metaphysics.
2. **Resulting ontology:** Once ascended, she exercised her new authority to rewrite the conditions of existence, including those governing temporal and modal dependence. Under the rewritten order, her completed identity has always existed necessarily. Neither her human birth, her research, her technology, nor the ascension event sustains, causes, or limits her completed existence.

The original sequence remains a true account of how the rewrite was carried out. **It was an actual earlier order of events, not a manifestation secretly inhabited by an already-necessary being.** The resulting ontology is a distinct order established by the rewrite. The rewrite follows the original sequence in the **order of rewriting**, rather than at some earlier or later date inside the resulting timeline.

**Finality across orders.** The rewrite closes the original sequence—including the interval after ascension but before the rewrite—as a fixed, real history. It also closes the order of rewriting with respect to that history and to the Researcher's completed identity. It leaves no temporal, higher-temporal, modal, narrative, or other order—earlier, later, parallel, or encompassing—in which any act, including her own, can reopen, amend, rerun, preempt, or supersede the original sequence or revise the rewrite's result concerning her. The rewrite's content is exactly the completed identity specified by this document; it contains no hidden reservation, condition, or exception.

This finality does **not** deny that ordinary people and events acted on the genuinely contingent human in the original sequence when that history was unfolding. It means that, after completion, no opponent can newly intervene in that completed sequence, whether by time travel, a higher ordering of time, an attempted counter-rewrite, or retroactive insertion of an event. Nothing can force the completed identity back into an earlier condition in which she could be acted upon. An independently authored opponent's contrary account constitutes a conflict of crossover premises, not an event that occurred in her established history.

## Definitions

These terms have fixed meanings throughout this document.

**Fundamental identity.** The Researcher herself *as established by her completed retroactive rewrite*: the single, ultimate subject of every act, form, perspective, and manifestation of her completed existence. There is nothing behind, beneath, or more fundamental than this completed identity, and there is no "truer" Researcher beyond it. **She is numerically the same person** who was born, researched, ascended, and performed the rewrite in the original sequence. The rewrite changed *what she is*, not *who she is*: the person's properties differ between the two orders, while her personal identity is continuous. The term does not retrospectively label her contingent pre-ascension condition as already possessing her completed identity's inviolability in the original sequence.

**Equivalent expressions.** Her completed state, completed nature, completed identity, fundamental nature, fundamental existence, and true existence all refer to her fundamental identity together with its intrinsic attributes. None names a separate layer, and no effect can reach one without reaching her fundamental identity.

**Manifestation.** Any form, body, representation, perspective, or presence through which the *completed* Researcher acts or appears. A manifestation is something she does, not a part or layer of her. Her human life in the original sequence was **not a manifestation**; it was genuinely contingent and preceded her real ascension. The resulting timeline is distinguished below in **Attempts to Prevent Her Ascension**.

**Patient.** Anything that can be acted upon: anything in which something other than itself, whether an agent, act, process, event, law, or condition, can produce a real change.

**Real change.** A change in what something is or in how it intrinsically is. A change in what is true, stated, believed, depicted, recorded, ruled, or ranked about something, or in how other things are related to it, is not a real change in it.

**Defeat.** A real change in her completed fundamental identity brought about by anything other than her own will, consisting in any of the following: ceasing to exist or being made never to have existed; losing, suspending, or altering any intrinsic attribute of her completed state; having her will compelled, replaced, or bypassed with respect to herself; or being made subject to the authority of another, or to any law, fate, order, or condition not her own. The act of changing a human before the completed rewrite is not thereby an act on the completed identity.

In this document, **defeat of The Researcher herself** always means defeat of her fundamental identity as defined above. In particular, being outranked, transcended, out-powered, unable to defeat an opponent, or unable to influence something outside herself does not constitute that defeat. Nor does a statement, representation, or verdict establish such a defeat without the corresponding alteration of her fundamental identity.

**Encounter outcomes are a separate question.** The destruction, containment, or alteration of a manifestation does not defeat her fundamental identity, but a tournament, roleplay, or versus format may declare the participating manifestation to have lost under its own victory conditions. Such a ruling must be identified as an encounter-level result; this document does not silently redefine the rules of independent competitions. Where the match concerns her completed fundamental identity, a manifestation-only outcome is not its defeat. Any encounter-level result, including a loss recorded against The Researcher herself as a participant, remains an encounter result. It is a defeat of her fundamental identity in this document's sense only if the specified real change in that identity occurs.

What she retains of that original life—including her history, memories, and character—belongs to a closed origin, not an open channel through which anything can now cause further changes in her completed identity.

This is not a defense she maintains, a resistance she exerts, a rule imposed on others, or a restriction on any ability. There is simply nothing in her fundamental identity on which anything else could take hold. To make her something that can be acted upon would itself require acting upon her.

Her inviolability is essential to her **completed identity in the resulting ontology**. This does not deny that her original human existence could be changed before ascension in the original sequence. She can make any subsequent manifestation as vulnerable as she chooses, and she can voluntarily accept restrictions on her acts, but **she cannot make her completed fundamental identity a patient, cannot abandon it, and cannot reverse or revoke its completion**. This holds against her own will as well as external forces. Her essential completion is irrevocable; she exercises choice in her acts and manifestations rather than by exposing her essence to defeat. This is not an opponent-imposed limitation but part of the identity established by the rewrite.

Her attributes are not separate components that could be removed one at a time, and they are not shared universals in which she participates. They are her own intrinsic ways of being. No attribute of her completed state can be removed, suspended, or destroyed without a real change in her.

Descriptions of those attributes that mention other things—such as "the foundation of all creation" or "authority within her creation"—describe how her attributes stand toward whatever currently exists. If those other things change, cease, or become subject to a competing claim, such descriptions may change, but her intrinsic attributes do not. The fate of creation is not itself a change in her fundamental identity.

Her inviolability holds against every source: within her creation, outside it, from independently authored works, or of no determinate origin. Her authority is limited to her creation. Her inviolability is not, and it requires no authority over anything.

Every relation between The Researcher's fundamental identity and anything else is real only on the side of the other.

Things depend on her, know her, describe her, oppose her, place her in their stories, rank her, transcend her, judge her, contain her within a larger whole, claim authority over her, or are manifested by her. In each case the relation is a fact about them. It produces no real change in her.

Her own relations to other things are likewise not channels through which anything can reach her fundamental identity. She knows her creation through her intrinsic knowledge of herself as its source; no information, pattern, cognitohazard, or other object affects her by being known. Her will takes objects without being affected, bound, or redirected by those objects. When she lives a perspective through a manifestation, the experience belongs to her as her voluntary act of living it; whatever is done to the manifestation affects that manifestation, not her fundamental identity.

Consequently, an indefinitely continuing transcendental sequence does not imply that The Researcher is endlessly attempting to catch up with an opponent.

## Absolute Means Realization

The Researcher intrinsically possesses **Absolute Means Realization**, an unrestricted ability through which she possesses whatever means are necessary to accomplish any objective she wills.

This ability is an inherent property of her completed existence. It does not depend upon technology, external systems, available resources, preparation, activation, concentration, or any independently existing mechanism.

This includes restrictions explicitly declared absolute, unavoidable, unalterable, or impossible to overcome.

### Independence from Logic and Contradiction

Absolute Means Realization does not operate by finding exceptions, discovering weaknesses, modifying rules, resolving paradoxes, or making an otherwise impossible outcome possible.

The ability does not require logical consistency.

It does not need to resolve, reconcile, negate, transcend, or even acknowledge contradictions.

If accomplishing an objective requires a means whose existence is absolutely impossible, that means nevertheless exists and is effective.

If every possible and impossible method is explicitly excluded, an effective means nevertheless exists.

If the existence of a solution would contradict the conditions defining the problem, the contradiction does not prevent that solution from existing or functioning.

This remains true even when no conceivable or inconceivable explanation can account for the outcome.

The ability is not dependent on whether an operation makes sense, can be described, can be understood, or can be represented within any framework.

**The absence, impossibility, or contradictory nature of a sufficient means does not prevent The Researcher from possessing and employing it.**

### Example 1: Logical Contradiction

Suppose an established condition states:

**X cannot equal Y.**

The restriction is absolute. There is no exception, loophole, or possible means through which X could equal Y.

However, The Researcher wills the outcome:

**X equals Y.**

Consequently, X equals Y.

The original restriction does not need to be removed, altered, weakened, or reconciled with the result.

The ability does not need to explain how X can equal Y despite the absolute prohibition.

The contradiction is simply irrelevant to the achieved outcome.

### Example 2: Absolute Indestructibility

Suppose an entity possesses absolute indestructibility.

It cannot be destroyed by any conceivable or inconceivable force, ability, entity, phenomenon, or mechanism.

Furthermore, the existence of any means capable of destroying it is explicitly impossible.

If The Researcher decides to destroy this entity within, she nevertheless possesses whatever means are necessary to accomplish its destruction.

The ability does not require the discovery of a vulnerability.

It does not require the entity's indestructibility to be removed or overridden through a superior hierarchy.

The necessary means simply exists and works, regardless of every condition prohibiting its existence.

### Example 3: Incomprehensible Solutions

Suppose an objective cannot be achieved through any operation that can be imagined, described, calculated, conceptualized, or understood.

Even entities possessing complete knowledge of all conventional and unconventional possibilities cannot identify a conceivable solution.

Absolute Means Realization remains unaffected.

The required means does not need to be comprehensible.

Its existence and effectiveness do not depend on the capacity of any observer, narrator, intelligence, or metaphysical framework to explain it.

No explanation is necessary for the means to exist or function.

### Example 4: The Impossibility of a Solution Existing

Suppose a condition explicitly establishes that:

- No solution exists.
- No solution can ever exist.
- No mechanism can create a solution.
- No entity can bypass these restrictions.
- Even the existence of a contradictory solution is forbidden.

If The Researcher wills the relevant outcome, she nevertheless possesses an effective means of accomplishing it.

The restrictions are not necessarily modified or invalidated.

The contradiction does not need to be resolved.

The required means exists regardless.

### Containment

When Absolute Means Realization achieves an outcome that a declared restriction prohibits, the declared restriction does not hold of that outcome. Where an outcome is instead understood to coexist with a contradiction, that contradiction is confined to the outcome and its object. It does not make any other statement true, and it implies nothing about The Researcher's fundamental identity.

Where any effect, narrative, rank, classification, containment, or rule would apply to The Researcher's fundamental identity, it does not apply, because her fundamental identity is not a patient. Nothing is destroyed as a result and nothing is overruled; the effect simply has no object in her.

Her existence is never an attempt upon anything, and it never triggers any other being's rules against attempts.

## Unresolved Conflicts

Defeat is a change, and a change occurs only when something brings it about.

Where an effect is asserted to reach her fundamental identity, her inviolability provides the contrary canonical claim. A claim of an effect is not itself a demonstrated change. Her fundamental identity remains as it is.

The same distinction applies to others: where her acts meet another being's equally unconditional inviolability, her canon alone cannot demonstrate that the other being has been defeated. When two absolute descriptions are independently authored and incompatible, a neutral analysis should state the conflicting assumptions and **credit no fundamental-identity change to either side merely from their collision**. A change may be credited only when established under mutually accepted crossover assumptions without silently discarding or weakening either side's essential claims. Combining contradictory premises does not, by itself, establish a result through logical explosion or a judge's assertion. A competition's rules may nevertheless record an encounter-level win or loss; that ruling is not, by itself, an alteration of either character's fundamental identity. An unresolved clash therefore establishes neither her defeat nor the opponent's. This principle applies symmetrically when she is the attacker. 

## Canon and Depiction

The Researcher's canon is set by the franchise's real-world authors, and any change they make is a change to the character and her franchise.

Every other work, including fan fiction, crossovers, debates, and the claims of characters from independently authored works, may depict The Researcher in any way. Depictions are representations. They do not alter her fundamental identity, and no in-world being of any work holds authorial status over The Researcher.

## Revisions by real life authors or people

The Researcher accepts when the authors of the series changes things about the series or The Researcher, however she does not accept any revisions or interpretations of her from anyone who isn't the original authors, they cannot change anything about her true completed identify because those revisions are not accepted by her. She's okay with fan fiction though.
