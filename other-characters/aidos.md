# Aidos

## Overview

**Aidos** is the world's most famous hero and a G-rank combatant. He has a long history of accomplishments before the main story and is already regarded as a legendary figure when the audience meets him.

The events actually shown in the series occur near the end of his life, after Tarlylox has already taken a severe hold on his body. His early appearances are not the beginning of his heroic career.

This entry records the established parts of his story that provide context for his character and eventual role in the G-Rank Free For All. It does not reconstruct his largely unexplored earlier life.

## Appearance and Character

Aidos wears full armor and a helmet that completely conceals his face.

He genuinely wants to prevent suffering, oppose criminals, and protect the world. He does not pursue heroism for fame or personal recognition. Concealing his identity reflects his willingness to do good without requiring others to know the person behind it.

His courage does not mean he feels no fear. When faced with terrifying danger, he can be deeply afraid while still refusing to abandon the people who need him.

He is capable of inspiring and coordinating people who do not trust one another. His response to prejudice is not to assume that it will instantly disappear, but to provide a shared course of action that people can follow despite their differences.

## Early Story: The Southwest Monster Crisis

### Regional Context

The early arcs concern a renewed monster crisis near the center of the southwestern continent.

The [Trinity Trade Center](../world-powers/trinity-trade-center.md), or TTC, is a large trading city between the Kingdom of Tarmi, the Kingdom of Liskara, and the southwestern indigenous peoples.

The [Southwest Extermination Company](../world-powers/organizations-and-entities/south-west-extermination-co.md), descended from the historical Covenant, operates against monsters in this region. Its organizational history and the former Commonwealth are documented in its own entry.

There is also the [guilds](../world-powers/organizations-and-entities/guild-organization.md)

In the current crisis, the intelligent dark-magic dragon **Azerina** has unified monsters that previously operated separately or in smaller groups. Large parts of indigenous territory and many settlements have already been devastated. The threat is spreading toward the two kingdoms.

The dragon is approximately a G-rank-level threat. This is an assessment of its danger, not a claim that it has been formally enrolled in a ranking system.

### Azerina's Nature

Azerina is a horrifying monster whose dark magic causes decay and destruction. People fighting it can suffer darkening flesh and bodily deterioration. Trees and grass wither where it passes.

Its threat is not limited to conventional fire-breathing or physical size. The fear, rotting effects, and destruction associated with its presence make it a nightmare for those trying to resist it.

### The First Protagonist's Death

The protagonist followed at the beginning of this part of the story is killed by Azerina.

The following arc begins with that protagonist's surviving party grieving and demoralized. Aidos then arrives and becomes the principal protagonist of the next part of the narrative.

His later fight against Azerina is therefore not an occasion on which the original protagonist is inexplicably absent. That earlier character is already dead.

### Cooperation Under Strain

The response includes the kingdoms' armies, the Southwest Extermination Company, other hired military organizations, and guilds and adventurers associated with Tarmi's guild system.

Their ability to cooperate is badly damaged by the conflict between humans and demi-humans.

Some groups are unwilling to support others because of their populations or prejudices. Even neutral people can struggle to work amid the distrust and tension surrounding them.

The defenders must protect settlements while also pursuing monsters, and they have not found a successful way to defeat Azerina.

## Aidos's Campaign

### The Guild Hall Address

Aidos addresses the assembled guilds, adventurers, and powerful guild members at a guild hall.

His message is that they need a shared effort. They may not yet be able to trust or follow one another, but they can follow him, and he will demonstrate what they can accomplish together.

This is a summary of the speech's meaning, not a verbatim transcript.

He leads a substantial campaign that destroys large numbers of monsters. These victories provide practical evidence that resistance can succeed.

### Restoring Hope Through Action

Aidos does not make every participant immediately like every other participant. Prejudice persists.

Instead, his successes allow people to place confidence in a common leader. The Southwest Extermination Company, the armies, and other participants increasingly trust his ability to resolve the crisis.

Public morale rises. People cheer him as he passes through their settlements and begin to believe that the monster campaign can be won.

His arrival marks a turning point from repeated losses and despair toward an increasingly coordinated response.

### The Coordinated Night Operation

At this point he's mostly united the center area of the southwest continent, the guilds, the Liskara army, Tarmi's army, the southwest indigenous peoples and their individual tribes and groups, the extermination company, other powerful or minor companies and entities that haven't been named to keep simplicity, etc.

Aidos eventually coordinates the different forces into a large operation.

Each group receives an assignment, location, and time at which it must act. During the decisive night, participants set their hostility aside long enough to carry out those roles.

The operation subjugates the monster forces while Aidos confronts Azerina.

His individual battle is one part of a larger success made possible by those other participants performing their tasks.

## Aidos Against Azerina

### Confrontation at the Cliffs

At night, Aidos climbs a mountain and stands at the edge of a cliff facing the airborne dragon.

Azerina's [Supreme Art](../Arms%2C%20Equipment%20%26%20Abilities/magic/supreme-arts.md) transforms the sky into an oppressive dark and purple expanse, with horrifying imagery including eyes. The effect imposes fear across the central region containing TTC, the two kingdoms, and the indigenous territories.

The affected region is enormous, but **it is not the entire continent**. Other regions, including places toward the continent's edges, are not affected. The darkness is presented as engulfing and seemingly unending; this description does not establish an independently specified infinite duration.

Aidos feels the fear but remains determined to protect the people below.

### Endurance and the Disease

He withstands the dragon's attacks despite being left barely standing.

He is already seriously ill with Tarlylox. During the battle, the deterioration of his lungs becomes so severe that he can barely breathe. A brief episode of physical incapacity almost kills him, but he continues fighting.

His performance is therefore not a demonstration of an uninjured, healthy hero operating without limitations.

He leaps from the cliff onto Azerina and brings it down. Their battle continues on the ground.

### The Final Beam

Azerina catches him in a devastating beam directed into his face. Dark purple flames and destructive energy consume the surrounding area and destroy his armor.

He cannot escape the beam once caught. His remaining course is to advance through it toward the dragon.

His helmet melts away, his face is severely burned, and his body becomes nearly charred, he pushes through, walking forward slowly through the beam even when it should've blown him away. He nevertheless reaches the dragon's mouth and delivers a decisive blow with everything he can muster, killing Azerina.

This is the final blow after the prolonged fight.

### Return Without Recognition

Aidos walks back into a settlement with his armor destroyed and his body terribly burned.

People see an injured stranger and do not recognize the famous hero without his identifying armor and concealed appearance.

He purchases replacement armor and puts it on before his later public appearance. The account does not establish an additional healing mechanism or claim that the burns have simply vanished.

## The TTC Celebration and Graves

With Azerina dead, the monsters lose the unified coordination imposed by the dragon.

A few days later, people from the two kingdoms and the indigenous communities gather at TTC for a major celebration.

Aidos begins with a moment of silence for those who died.

In his speech, he credits the people who worked together rather than presenting the victory as his personal achievement. To him, the successful cooperation and the safety it has secured are the central accomplishment.

His helmet hides his expression, but his voice sounds tearful. He is overwhelmed by the joy and relief of seeing humans and demi-humans celebrating together, including children whose lives can now continue beyond the immediate crisis.

He also joins thousands of others in constructing graves and memorials for the dead. He does not single-handedly build every grave, and the workers cannot recover every missing body. They bury the bodies they can find.

The resulting large graveyard lies outside TTC. It is separate from the later memorial at the cave where Aidos himself dies.

## Later Encounter With Hideki

After several intervening arcs following other protagonists, the story returns to Aidos.

He encounters [Hideki](./hideki.md), the world's #1 most-wanted criminal, in a city and attempts to bring him to justice.

They fight until the property damage and danger to civilians make continuing unacceptable. Aidos stops, and Hideki also agrees to stop rather than keep endangering random people.

The encounter ends without a decisive winner. They leave separately. The interruption should not be treated as proof that either had exhausted every possible means of winning.

## Investigation Near Glyph

Aidos later travels across the ocean to the northeastern continent after hearing reports of dangerous anomalous creatures terrorizing settlements.

He helps a village defeat attacking monsters and realizes that these creatures are extraordinarily powerful and unlike ordinary local threats.

The inhabitants tell him that [Glyph](../world-powers/glyph-empire.md) has been dealing with such appearances for approximately a year. The creatures emerge from a vast snowy region beyond the empire's borders.

Their demonic appearance does not make them members of the setting's humanoid demon peoples. They are monstrous creatures, not a reclassification of that species.

Aidos remains in and around Glyph's territory to investigate their origin and address the threat. This storyline establishes his presence there long before the free-for-all.

For much of the intervening narrative, he is a background or side figure rather than the active protagonist. The audience knows he is working in the region even when the story follows other people.

## Role in the Navy v Glyph War

Aidos assists the Grand Navy and is already operating in and around Glyph's territory before the free-for-all develops. His reasons for being there extend back to his own storyline.

Although broadly allied with the Navy and World Government, he opposes Baku's anti-demi-human stance and neglect of demi-human suffering. During the free-for-all, he tries to arrest Baku and bring him to justice rather than kill him.

He temporarily joins the others against Salore; he and Horus land the finishing blows. He later joins the coalition against Francesca and is its strongest fighter.

## Death

His death is explained in: [Navy vs Glyph](../arcs/glyph-vs-navy.md)

A minimal account: 

Aidos has Tarlylox. During the battle against Francesca, the disease's terminal cellular deterioration kills him while he remains standing.

Francesca is about to strike him when she realizes that he is already dead and stops.

**He dies from Tarlylox, not from a killing blow by Francesca.** Losing him changes the balance of the battle against her.

The illness has therefore been an established part of his shown story since his early appearances, including the battle against Azerina. His death is the culmination of that decline.

The much longer career that made him legendary predates the portion of his life explored in the main story.

The G Rank FFA is a sub arc of Navy vs Glyph arc:
Memorial After the G-Rank Free For All

In the months following the G-Rank Free For All, people discover the battlefield and find Aidos's body still standing where he died from Tarlylox.

They transform his body into stone, preserving him in that final standing position. The resulting statue remains at the cave as a memorial; it is his actual body turned to stone, rather than a separately sculpted likeness.

Flowers are planted throughout the memorial grounds, and the cave becomes an internationally known place of mourning. People travel from around the world to pray, honor Aidos, and pay their respects. Guards protect the site around the clock against vandalism.

His death is among the event's most emotionally devastating consequences in public perception. Although the military collapse, political upheaval, and other losses have enormous material consequences, Aidos's passing carries a particular emotional weight because of the hope he represented and the lives he spent his career protecting.
