# Demon King Malu (The Original Demon King)

Before the main events of the story, this is established history of the past. It is not directly shown in the story. Centuries ago, Malu existed and was the Original Demon King. This is like the classic demon king you see in fantasy, they waant to conquer everything etc.

Malu is an evil for the sake of evil type of character, actively seeking out suffering. 

Narratively, Malu represents the incarnation of Evil itself.

Malu only has a .md instead of a folder for his character because he isn't described much, all the audience should know really is that in the past of the setting's history which honestly is barely explored in the story, he tried to conquer the world and enslave everybody to make them suffer, was defeated by a party of powerful G rank heros. Malu himself is a G rank as well, however he is more suited to have his own rank ABOVE G rank, it took multiple G ranks to take him down and they barely won. This is similar to the G Rank Free For All where Francesca (as Pheme / After being fired from the Navy, but Pre end game), a G rank, fought multiple G ranks and won. Arguably, it seems like history repeated itself. Theses are the only 2 times in history this has ever happened. 

Malu appears in flashbacks and legends and myths in the main story where people talk about him, but not oftenly shown in the story. Had the party not defeated him, the story in this series would likely not take place because everybody dies. 

Malu only truly appears in the series once, during the final arcs of the entire series where he appears to fight Francesca. More information is in the arcs folder of this repo. 

During this fight Malu and Francesca showed feats that could be considered at the high end of G ranks, or possibly higher.

During the sequel story where they have a rematch (so if you look in the sequel story folder at their events), they fight at a level that is so high we would give them their own tier about G rank. 

Malu is a [vengeful spirit](../Arms,%20Equipment%20&%20Abilities/vengeful-spirits.md). He is originally a demon, however after his defeat, his will for revenge was so powerful that he became a vengeful spirit.

The way Malu appears during the final arcs in the series to fight Francesca, is that the [contemporary Demon King](./demon-king-orca.md) of the series, uses the [Black Ring](../Arms,%20Equipment%20&%20Abilities/black-ring.md) to call upon Malu for help, prompting Malu to possess the contemporary Demon King's body, allowing him to take full control for a certain time limit. Through the possessed body, Malu fights Francesca.

During their rematch, Malu actually resurrects, that is in the sequel though. The mechanics are explained better there.

Any feats or abilities of Malu are not specified here, they would likely be shown in arcs where he is documented / events, that described how heh fights. But none are shown here in this file.
