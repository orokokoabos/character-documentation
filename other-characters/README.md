# Other Characters

these are characters other than Francesca, we document a few but not all of them. Because this repo is supposed to be about Francesca, so we only documented other characters who are relevant to explaining Francesca or can give additional context to Francesca related situations.
for example, there is a main character who is a chef in the series, however his part in the show has NOTHING to do with Francesca, therefore he is NOT included/documented in this repo. 
So basically there are hundreds of characters that we have not documented here, all the characters inside this folder are only characters who have some relevancy when it comes to talking about Francesca, any charcaters that has nothing to do with Francesca (which is most of them) are not included here and you will not see them in this folder.
