# Power Ranks

## How rankings emerged
In the Southwest part of the world, the Kingdom of Tarmi had a dungeon and monster issue, so they developed the Guild System, where you slay monsters and defeat dungeons for money, bringing in people around the world. It was very famous. And it was only a thing in Tarmi and it's surrounding territory. To show or rank how strong a slayer / guild member was, they used ranks to divide them. This tradition was used only for these guild systems, however they got very famous, and many people around the world liked the idea, and people started using it as a ranking / status thing around the world. It is not official everywhere though. In some places they might treat it as official and very important, some places might not care, some might have it just for fun. In the current setting's story, the Guild System still exists, and is popular, people travel from around the world to come to Tarmi to participate and potentially make money in it. But it is not a thing established worldwide, just the tradition of it / culture of it is well known, so it's real in the guild system, but worldwide, it's more of a culture thing. 

## Ranks

### F Rank

Ordinary human, not very capable, average or below average. Note: an "ordinary average human" in this story's setting, is greatly above humans in REAL LIFE. If you compare an actual IRL human to an average human in the series, they cannot compare. If you take the strongest, peak athletic human in the real life, and compare it to an average or below average human in this series' setting, the human in this series likely outclasses the IRL human.

### E Rank

Average. Unreliable, maybe block level in general.

### D Rank

Much more reliable, maybe village level in general.

### C Rank

Maybe town level. Above average ability, where most people with experience land.

### B Rank

Arguably city level, small city. Very capable and respected, where most people land if they train for years or are naturally gifted, and extremely hard to become. They are very powerful and respected in society.

### A Rank

Large city level, this is the highest most people can go. This is the dream for basically almost everybody, it is extremely rare, and huge celebrations are thrown everytime there is a new A rank. They are basically celebrities honestly. This is what people aspire to be. Imagine 99% (general number to show / illustrate a point, not the real statistic) of people never are able to reach this level.

### S Rank

Large city level to metropolis level. Any S rank is basically famous, there are only about 100 - 200 S ranks in the main story, out of billions in the entire world. The reason "A Rank" is the dream, is because becoming S rank is unrealistic, you're not gonna become an S rank, they are that powerful.

### SS Rank

Metropolis level. Only a dozen exist in the world. And under 50 exist in the history of the entire story's setting.

### SSS Rank

Large Metropolis level. Only a dozen exist in the world. And under 50 exist in the history of the entire story's setting.

### G Rank

Potentially national level. Only about under 50 G ranks exist in the history of the entire story's setting. 

### Above G Rank

## Why the power levels are misleading

If we say "large city level", maybe you can think we mean they can destroy a city easily, but it really depends. A mage with a huge AOE can destroy an entire large city easily if they are S rank, but another S rank, focused on hand to hand combat, who has no large AOE and cannot destroy an entire city, could reasonably defeat the mage in a 1v1. When taken by just their destructive power, the hand to hand S rank would not be an S rank, however in a 1v1 fight, they defeat the mage, but the mage is an S rank, so it would be unreasonable to say the hand to hand fighter is below an S rank. 

Another example, Salore is a G rank, fighting him is basically a death sentence, we could put him a national level, HOWEVER he fights with weapons and physical ability, no magic. He doesn't have the destructive power to wipe out a nation instantly, however a nation sending multiple individuals after him one by one, would lose. If we take a nation and sends millions to fight him, given enough time, he individually defeats everybody, but it takes a very very long time, he doesn't wipe them out instantly. But a G rank with powerful AOE? Could wipe a nation out instantly (although this mage doesn't exist, just an example). So that is why using the "levels" like "town level", "nation level", can be misleading. It does not automatically mean they can wipe out an entire nation or city instantly, it depends on what you're abilities actually are. However it is useful for people to get the general idea of how strong you are. Like Salore cannot wipe out a nation instantly, however he is a national threat. A mage who has ability to AOE wipe out a city, may be extremley inexperienced in anything else, and lose to a group of C ranks, would yuo call this mage an S rank? No, he could wipe the city out, but his skill in other things is very lacking and one trick doesn't solve it, so again, levels are misleading, but we use them nevertheless to give a visual representation / rough idea of how dangerous a person is.
