# Guns & Firearms

## Overview

Guns and firearms within the setting are divided into three primary categories:

1. **Guns**
2. **Accialite Powered Guns (A.P.G.)**
3. **Cursed-Man's Guns (C.M.G.)**

These categories differ primarily in how they generate the energy required to fire.

Although all three are classified as guns and firearms, their operating principles are substantially different.

**The term "Guns" is also the proper name of one specific firearm category. It does not exclusively refer to firearms in general.**

---

## 1. Guns

### Overview

**Guns** are a category of magical catalyst firearms that use the wielder's own magical energy to accelerate physical ammunition.

Guns in real life use gunpowder, but this is guns in this series' fictional settting, and these guns do not use gunpowder. We do have an equivalent to IRL guns though, those are C.M.Gs.

Instead, the user channels magical energy directly into the weapon.

That energy provides the force necessary to propel a projectile out of the barrel.

### Operating Mechanism

A standard Gun contains two primary components.

**1. Ammunition**

Guns use physical projectiles.

These can include metal slugs, shaped bullets, and specialized ammunition.

The ammunition may be stored in magazines, cylinders, or internal chambers, depending on the weapon's construction.

**2. Channeling Core**

The channeling core allows magical energy to pass through the weapon.

It may contain runes or conduits that facilitate the transfer of magic.

A stronger or more efficient channeling core allows the weapon to make better use of the energy supplied by its wielder.

### Firing

When the trigger is pulled, the wielder channels their own magic through the weapon's core.

The channeled magical energy accelerates the ammunition out of the barrel.

Consequently, the strength of the shot depends heavily on the individual using the gun.

### Dependence on the Wielder

A Gun's performance is directly influenced by the user's magical capabilities.

A weak or untrained user may produce relatively slow, unstable, or low-impact shots.

A skilled user with substantial magical capacity can operate the same firearm with far greater precision and firepower.

Therefore, the same weapon can perform very differently depending on who wields it.

### Production and Scalability

Guns are comparatively inexpensive to produce relative to Cursed-Man's Guns.

They are also scalable in the sense that their effectiveness can increase substantially with the user's own magical capabilities and experience.

However, because they depend on the wielder's magic, they are not suitable for individuals who cannot supply the required magical energy.

### Difference From Accialite Powered Guns

Standard Guns use magic supplied directly by the wielder.

Accialite Powered Guns instead use magical energy already stored within Accialite.

This distinction is fundamental to the difference between the two categories.

## 2. Accialite Powered Guns (A.P.G.)

### Overview

**Accialite Powered Guns (A.P.G.s)** are a category of magical firearms that use energy stored within Accialite as their source of firepower.

They are considered the strongest of the three established firearm categories in terms of potential firepower.

Unlike standard Guns, they do not rely on the user continuously supplying their own magic as the primary source of the weapon's power.

Instead, their firepower comes from the magical energy stored within an Accialite core.

For information about the material itself, see [Accialite](achilite.md).

### Operating Principle

Accialite possesses the ability to absorb and store magical energy.

An A.P.G. incorporates this material into its construction.

The firearm then uses the stored magical energy to produce powerful shots.

The precise internal release mechanism has not yet been documented.

### Factors Affecting Firepower

The strength of an A.P.G. depends primarily on two factors.

**1. Accialite Core Size**

The amount of Accialite contained within the weapon affects its potential capabilities.

**2. Stored Magic Potency**

The potency of the magical energy stored within the Accialite directly influences the weapon's firepower.

A weapon containing more potent magical energy can produce substantially stronger attacks.

### Firepower

A sufficiently powerful A.P.G. can surpass the firepower of firearms belonging to either of the other two categories.

This gives A.P.G.s exceptionally high destructive potential.

However, an individual A.P.G.'s exact capabilities depend on its construction and stored magical energy.

Not every A.P.G. should automatically be assumed to possess identical firepower.

### Charging Time

The primary disadvantage of A.P.G.s is the substantial amount of time required to charge their Accialite.

This makes charging an important logistical consideration.

The ability to store magical energy indefinitely is useful, but obtaining and storing the necessary energy can be time-consuming.

### Historical Development

Accialite Powered Guns were invented during the **Expansion Era (Chimera War)**.

Their development took place at a research facility in **Das Grenzgold (Fortress)**.

They represent an important military application of Accialite's magical energy storage properties.

## 3. Cursed-Man's Guns (C.M.G.)

### Overview

**Cursed-Man's Guns (C.M.G.s)** are a category of conventional firearms that use gunpowder and mechanical firing mechanisms.

Unlike Guns and Accialite Powered Guns, C.M.G.s do not rely on magical energy to operate.

They were developed for individuals who could not use magic.

Their technology is comparable to IRL firearms such as muskets and older rifles.

### Origin of the Name

The inventors of Cursed-Man's Guns regarded individuals unable to use magic as "cursed.". They were religious and believed that God gives and blesses us with magic, so if you couldn't use magic, you are cursed and unworthy.

This attitude influenced the name of the firearm category.

Consequently, the term **Cursed-Man's Gun** reflects the social prejudice surrounding people who cannot use magic.

The term "cursed-man" can function as a derogatory label but mostly inside the Divine Asrien Kingdom, as they are very classist when it comes to using magic considering they are a magic focused kingdom, treating magic as a god given gift in their religion.

### Operating Mechanism

C.M.G.s are genuine mechanical firearms.

They use physical ammunition, gunpowder, and mechanical firing mechanisms.

Their shots do not require magical energy.

As long as the user possesses functional equipment, gunpowder, ammunition, and the physical ability to operate the weapon, they can fire it without using magic.

### Common Weapon Types

C.M.G.s commonly appear as relatively simple firearms.

Examples include:

- Long rifles.
- Muskets.
- Pistols.

### Independence From Magical Ability

The effectiveness of a C.M.G.'s firing mechanism does not depend on the user's magical capacity.

An individual completely incapable of using magic can still operate one.

Likewise, having extraordinary magical reserves does not inherently increase the weapon's firepower.

This differs from standard Guns, whose performance can scale with the wielder's magical abilities.

### Strengths

**Accessibility**

C.M.G.s enable individuals without magical abilities to participate in armed combat.

**Resistance to Anti-Magic**

Because their firing mechanisms do not depend on magic, C.M.G.s are not disabled simply by effects that interfere with magical energy.

**Reliability**

Their destructive capabilities arise from physical ammunition and conventional firearms technology.

Consequently, their effectiveness does not depend on the wielder successfully channeling magic.

### Weaknesses

**Reloading**

Older C.M.G.s, especially muskets and certain pistols, can require lengthy or complicated reloading procedures.

**Logistics**

C.M.G.s require supplies of physical ammunition and gunpowder.

Their operation therefore depends on maintaining these supplies.

Unlike standard Guns, they cannot rely solely on the magical energy supplied by the wielder to accelerate their ammunition.

### Social Perception

In societies dominated by magic users, C.M.G.s may be regarded as crude, noisy, or barbaric.

One example is **the Divine Asrien Dominion**.

The prejudice associated with the term "cursed-man" reflects the social position of individuals who cannot use magic.

However, C.M.G.s have demonstrated practical usefulness in warfare.

### Military Applications

Military units consisting primarily of non-magic users can employ C.M.G.s in organized firing ranks.

These formations can provide meaningful battlefield resistance even against opponents with access to magic.

C.M.G.s may also serve as backup firearms when magical resources are unavailable or depleted.

Some soldiers carry both C.M.G.s and magic-powered Guns when they have access to both types of equipment.
