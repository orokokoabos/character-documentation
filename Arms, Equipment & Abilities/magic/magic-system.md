# Magic System

## Overview

Magic within the setting operates through extraordinary particles commonly referred to as **mana**.

Although most civilizations believe that mana is an innate resource possessed by living beings, this understanding is incorrect.

Mana originates from an external source: an extraordinary sphere managed by an entity known as **The Researcher**.

When living beings attempt to perform magic, they unknowingly request particles from this sphere. The particles are supplied to them, and the individual temporarily controls and shapes them to produce magical phenomena.

This process is the underlying basis of magic across different species and civilizations.

Despite differences in appearance, terminology, religious interpretation, and methods of training, the underlying magical resource is the same.

**This explanation represents information available through external lore rather than knowledge possessed by ordinary characters within the main story.**

## 1. The Mana Sphere

The original source of mana is a perfect sphere existing outside the universe containing the main story.

The sphere belongs to a larger external reality.

It contains an extraordinarily large quantity of particles whose properties differ fundamentally from those of ordinary matter.

Although these particles are conventionally called mana, that is not necessarily their original or technically accurate designation.

### Incompressibility and Internal Pressure

The particles are described as incompressible.

The sphere must maintain some unoccupied capacity rather than becoming completely filled.

An illustrative model uses a remaining empty or compressible volume fraction represented by epsilon, written as ε.

The original explanation compares this to a sealed container containing incompressible material and a small compressible pressure buffer.

In that idealized comparison:

\[
P=\frac{nRT}{\varepsilon V}
\]

As ε approaches zero, the modeled pressure approaches infinity.

The purpose of this analogy is to illustrate why the sphere must avoid reaching complete fullness.

The formula is a conceptual explanation rather than a fully established physical equation governing the sphere's actual exotic particles.

The exact consequences of the sphere reaching 100% capacity remain unknown.

The Researcher deliberately prevents this condition.

### Releasing Particles

The sphere does not automatically regulate itself.

The Researcher manages the system by directing particles away from the sphere and into other locations.

This reduces the quantity of particles currently contained within it.

One such release was responsible for triggering the expansion and formation of the universe in which the main narrative takes place.

The universe's creation is therefore connected to the Researcher's management of the particles.

However, the universe is not the only destination for mana.

Particles are distributed to numerous locations.

## 2. The Researcher's Role

The system is associated with **The Researcher**, a woman who originated from an earlier human civilization.

She developed capabilities through technological advancement that far exceeded those of ordinary civilizations.

Her relationship with the sphere is experimental.

She oversees the distribution and return of its particles.

The precise motivation for creating or maintaining this experiment has not been explained.

The Researcher's present nature, authority, and metaphysical properties are documented separately in her character files.

**The older description of the experiment does not supersede the Researcher's more recent character documentation.**

### Knowledge of the Sphere

No one in the main narrative is known to understand the sphere's existence or the true origin of mana.

The Researcher herself knows about the system.

However, the information is not explained within the main story.

The intended distinction is between what the audience can learn through external lore and what the characters themselves know.

Even characters who correctly question conventional theories of magic do not necessarily know about the sphere, the experiment, or the Researcher.

## 3. Properties of Mana Particles

Mana particles possess extraordinary properties that distinguish them from conventional matter.

They can:

- Travel in ways that disregard ordinary relativistic limitations, effectively reaching destinations instantaneously.
- Phase through ordinary physical barriers and between realities.
- Temporarily produce alterations to physical reality.
- Respond to the intentions of individuals receiving them.
- Distinguish between different kinds of phenomena with a degree of intelligence or contextual judgment.

The particles should not automatically be interpreted as fully conscious individuals.

Their intelligence does not necessarily involve thoughts, personalities, emotions, or human-like reasoning.

Instead, they possess enough awareness or discrimination to carry out complicated functions that cannot be reduced to completely rigid, simplistic rules.

This characteristic is especially important for certain supernatural weapons.

## 4. Requesting Mana

When a living being intends to perform magic, that intention functions as a request for mana.

The individual does not need to consciously ask the sphere for particles.

For example, a human preparing to cast a fireball might simply think about creating and projecting fire.

Because the intended action requires mana, that intention also serves as an implicit request for the necessary particles.

The particles respond to the request and appear at the appropriate location.

From the individual's perspective, they are simply using their magical abilities.

They generally do not realize that an external transaction has occurred.

### Two Fundamental Processes

Most conventional spellcasting involves two underlying processes:

**1. Requesting mana**

The individual obtains particles from the external sphere through the intention to perform a magical action.

**2. Shaping and controlling mana**

The individual temporarily controls the supplied particles and directs their properties toward a desired result.

These are distinct processes, even though most magic users are unaware of the distinction.

A person can become more effective at magic by improving either process.

## 5. The Misconception of Innate Mana

The prevailing belief among most inhabitants of Earth is that living beings naturally possess mana within their bodies.

Under this interpretation, magical training consists of learning to control and release an internal resource.

However, this explanation is incorrect.

Mana is supplied externally when needed.

The apparent immediacy of this process makes the misconception understandable.

Particles can arrive so quickly that the caster cannot ordinarily distinguish an external request from the activation of an internal ability.

The conventional belief that mana is naturally stored within living bodies therefore persists across many civilizations.

## 6. Human Magic

Humans generally emphasize practice, training, and technical control when developing magical abilities.

A human spellcaster may repeatedly practice creating fireballs, controlling projectiles, producing energy blasts, or performing other magical actions.

From their perspective, they are improving their ability to manipulate their supposedly innate mana.

In reality, their practice develops their ability to request mana and shape the particles they receive.

Human training tends to emphasize the manipulation and application of particles rather than deliberately maximizing the quantity requested.

This is a broad cultural tendency rather than a universal rule governing every human.

## 7. Demon Magic

Demons often possess a different cultural understanding of magic.

Many believe that their magical abilities originate from a demon god or other religious source.

Their magical practices therefore emphasize prayer, devotion, and requests for power.

However, the particles underlying their magic come from the same external sphere used by humans.

### Why Demon Magic Is Often Stronger

A demon repeatedly praying for power may unknowingly become very effective at requesting mana.

Through religious practice, they develop the ability to obtain comparatively large quantities of particles.

Consequently, demon magic is often stronger than ordinary human magic because demons may receive a greater supply of mana.

This is a tendency, not an absolute distinction between species.

Individual variation, training, and other circumstances can produce exceptions.

### Why Demon Magic Looks Different

Demons may also visualize magical effects differently from humans.

For example, a basic magical energy attack produced by a human might manifest as a blue shockwave.

A demon using the same underlying type of magic might produce a red-and-black electrical-looking discharge.

This difference does not establish that the two attacks derive from different kinds of particles.

Instead, cultural expectations and visualization influence how the particles are shaped.

The resulting magical phenomena can therefore look dramatically different despite sharing the same fundamental origin.

## 8. Other Species and Traditions

The same principle extends beyond humans and demons.

Different species and civilizations may possess their own explanations for magic.

They may use different rituals, techniques, terminology, and training methods.

Some may consider their magic fundamentally distinct from that of other species.

However, these traditions ultimately operate through the same system of requesting and shaping mana particles.

Their differences arise from how they access, visualize, and control the shared resource.

## 9. Individuals Unable to Use Magic

Some individuals cannot successfully obtain or use mana through conventional spellcasting.

One possible explanation is that they cannot make an effective request.

Another is that the sphere's system rejects their requests.

The precise reasons for this limitation remain undetermined.

An older hypothesis suggests that restrictions might help the system maintain the necessary balance between particle release and return.

However, this balancing explanation is not confirmed.

Individuals unable to use magic may still be able to operate equipment powered by other mechanisms, including conventional firearms and certain externally supplied magical devices.

## 10. Return of Mana Particles

(Mana particles stored inside [Achilite](../../Arms%2C%20Equipment%20%26%20Abilities/achilite.md) do not return because it counts as being in use and not ready for return yet)

Mana particles do not remain outside the sphere forever.

At some point, an individual particle phases out of its current location and returns to the sphere.

This can occur after a variable interval.

For example, residual particles remaining in an area after a battle might persist for a day, a week, a month, or some other duration.

There is no established universal return schedule.

However, the particles eventually return.

### Conservation of Particles

Mana particles are not continually created or destroyed through ordinary use.

The same overall collection of particles is repeatedly distributed and recovered.

However, the total number is unimaginably large.

Even across billions of years, the proportion of distinct particles used can remain extraordinarily small compared with the total number available.

### Maintaining the Sphere's Capacity

Because particles eventually return, the Researcher must continue distributing them to prevent excessive accumulation within the sphere.

Numerous locations serve as destinations.

Earth is only one of these outlets.

The use of magic by living beings is therefore part of a much larger process of particle distribution and return.

## 11. Magical Objects and Stored Energy

Not every magical item operates in exactly the same manner.

Some equipment uses magic channeled by its wielder.

Other equipment draws upon magical energy already stored within a material or device.

For example, [Accialite](achilite.md) can absorb and retain magical energy, allowing it to power equipment such as [Accialite Powered Guns](guns.md).

The detailed relationship between stored magical energy and the eventual return of individual mana particles still requires clarification.

### Self-Sustaining Magical Contracts

A particularly rare type of magical object operates through a self-sustaining request for mana.

Ordinarily, requests arise from the intentions of living beings.

However, extraordinarily advanced magic can imitate the relevant characteristics of conscious intention closely enough to produce a valid request.

Such a spell does not necessarily possess genuine consciousness.

Rather, it mimics the characteristics necessary for the mana system to recognize a request.

A properly constructed self-sustaining magical contract can therefore repeatedly obtain mana without requiring the wearer or owner to personally make each request.

The exact restrictions and capabilities of these objects remain to be established.

## 12. Spell Scrolls and Reversed Spells

Spell scrolls are objects containing magical instructions.

Their activation requires access to sufficient magical energy, whether supplied by the user or another available source.

For example, travelers in desert regions may carry one-use scrolls capable of producing drinking water.

### Spell Reversal

Some spells can be reversed when the opposite effect can be clearly conceptualized and controlled.

For example, a healing spell that creates or restores tissue may have an understandable reversal that damages or destroys tissue.

However, not every spell possesses an equally clear opposite.

A fireball does not necessarily have one uniquely defined reversal. An iceball, for example, is not automatically established as its opposite.

Reversal therefore depends on whether the opposite operation is sufficiently coherent and can be performed through the relevant magical control.

These principles are drawn from the older explanation and can be refined independently of any particular character's later equipment.

## 13. Intelligent Interpretation of Supernatural Phenomena

An especially important property of mana particles is their ability to evaluate whether a phenomenon should be considered supernatural.

This determination is not necessarily a simple binary classification based on the terminology used by a society.

The particles can distinguish between contexts and make judgments regarding unusual phenomena.

For example, human or demon magic is ordinarily considered supernatural and can be treated accordingly.

In a hypothetical crossover involving another fictional setting, a phenomenon might be described as a natural part of that setting's universe.

However, the particles might still interpret it as supernatural.

Their judgment is therefore not strictly determined by how the phenomenon's original users describe it.

This property provides the underlying mechanism for certain supernatural-nullification weapons.

## 14. The Supernatural-Phenomena Nullifier

One known application of mana's interpretive properties is a weapon contained within [Salore's Weapon Bag](salores-weapon-bag.md).

The weapon uses mana to identify and nullify supernatural phenomena.

Its detailed mechanics are documented in the weapon bag's entry.

The important principle is that the mana particles themselves evaluate the encountered phenomenon and determine whether it qualifies for nullification.

The ability is therefore not simply a physical cutting or destructive effect.

The following subjects require additional confirmation:

- The exact physical and metaphysical behavior of the mana sphere.
- The consequences of reaching complete capacity.
- The Researcher's current relationship to the historical experiment.
- The detailed rules governing requests that are rejected.
- The precise limits of intelligent particle classification.
- The relationship between persistent magical energy storage and particle return.
- The detailed operation of self-sustaining magical contracts.
- The broader limitations of spell reversal.

The Researcher's present characterization and Francesca's end-game abilities should be governed by their separately updated documentation rather than automatically importing every detail from the older explanation.

**The central established concept is that magic consists of externally supplied particles that living beings unknowingly request and temporarily shape, while the Researcher manages their distribution and return as part of an experiment beyond the ordinary characters' knowledge.**
