# Salore's Weapon Bag

**Salore's Weapon Bag** is an ancient black duffel bag with an effectively infinite, physically accessible interior. It holds dozens of supernatural weapons and utilities, ordinary possessions, two long-term living inhabitants, and a largely empty kingdom of hundreds of buildings. This page documents **23 selected contents**, not everything in the bag. Most items have no established proper name and are identified here by their functions.

Salore, the **world's fourth-most-wanted criminal**, bought the bag for an enormous price. After he dies in the **G-Rank Free For All**, **Francesca Badu** takes it from his body and immediately uses several of its weapons against the remaining G-rank combatants. She keeps it afterward, including into her end-game period at approximately **23 years old**, but ordinarily **does not carry or use it**.

## Origin and ownership

Centuries before the main story, a **vampire monarch** governed a province under [Demon King Malu](../other-characters/demon-king-malu). He created the bag as a potential military logistics tool: soldiers could transport practically unlimited supplies, equipment, and weapons, with possible applications in espionage and covert transportation. The project was never widely implemented because putting it into practice demanded too much work. The bag survived, passed through successive owners, and was eventually offered for sale at an extremely high price. Salore purchased it; the precise price, rediscovery circumstances, and intermediate ownership history are unknown.

Salore used the bag both as an **arsenal** and an everyday storage container. Its goose and black mamba were already living inside before he bought it. After Salore's death in the G-Rank Free For All, Francesca **takes** the bag rather than receiving it through a formal inheritance. She fights with its contents during that same battle, retains it afterward, and occasionally adds belongings of her own. Ownership does not imply that she habitually uses every item or even brings the bag to later fights.

## Interior, kingdom, and ordinary contents

The bag's exterior resembles an ordinary large black duffel bag; its exact dimensions and construction are unspecified. Its interior is an enormous, apparently infinite space that people and other living creatures can physically enter, move through, and potentially inhabit. The precise entrance/exit mechanics and environmental conditions have not been explained.

An **immortal goose**, resident for centuries, has built an entire **kingdom of hundreds of buildings** inside. The buildings are generally **empty**: the goose loves construction and is not housing a population or governing a functioning state. Building inside the bag also protects its work from outsiders who might occupy, interfere with, or destroy structures built in the ordinary world. It produces its own building materials, uses the **Infinite Vegetation Spray Bottle** for landscaping, and maintains its own network of **Teleportation Ink** destinations between buildings and projects. The bag also contains a substantial **library** stocked partly with books Salore collects for the goose. It is theoretically possible for people to enter and live in the buildings, though no such populated settlement is established.

Salore stores ordinary items here too: **money, clothing, luggage, travel supplies, food, water, books, household possessions, and dismantled furniture** such as tables and chairs he can reassemble after moving. He may pack luggage normally and put the entire case inside. Stored objects are not automatically protected from the inhabitants: the black mamba might eat food Salore intended to retrieve later. Francesca adds **makeup, cosmetics, and miscellaneous personal belongings** after taking ownership. Many further mundane and supernatural objects exist but are not individually inventoried.

## Selected contents

The numbering below is an **index**, not a ranking, acquisition order, or claim that these are the only contents.

### 1. Unstoppable-Projectile Sniper Rifle

**Rule:** Once fired, each bullet **must continuously preserve its original velocity** until the firing user decides it should stop. It cannot be stopped or slowed by water, earth, barriers, or ordinary resistance. It could travel through the Earth, into space, or beyond the universe while the rule remains active. If the user **dies or becomes incapable of giving the stop instruction**, the projectile automatically stops. The exact stop-command method is unspecified.

**Reality correction:** If the bullet meets an otherwise indestructible wall, the wall cannot be allowed to halt it. Reality therefore creates a bullet-sized passage through the wall. Likewise, a supposedly indestructible person's head can be penetrated along its path. This is **not ordinary infinite damage**: the projectile's continued movement is enforced by reality, and matter must yield enough for that movement to remain possible.

**Limits:** The bullet is not guaranteed to hit or kill a chosen target. For instance, a target separated by an effectively infinite intervening distance could remain unreachable even while the bullet moves continuously. Whether an outside effect can redirect the bullet **without changing its speed**, or whether its entire direction must also remain fixed, has not been conclusively defined. Its interaction with the Black Flag is specified under item 15.

### 3. Vector-Manipulating Sword

#### Overview

The **Vector-Manipulating Sword** is a supernatural sword capable of manipulating movement vectors within a field surrounding the weapon.

The field is somewhat comparable to a magnetic field, although it manipulates movement rather than ordinary magnetic forces.

Its primary function is to change how selected targets move, allowing the wielder to redirect an opponent's attempted movements in ways that make avoiding the sword extremely difficult.

**The wielder is exempt from the field's effects.**

Crucially, the field does **not** automatically reverse or redirect every movement occurring inside it. The wielder determines which targets and movements are affected.

#### How Vector Manipulation Works

A vector describes a quantity with both direction and magnitude. In the context of this sword, the most important application is the manipulation of an opponent's direction of movement.

For example, an opponent might attempt to move backward, away from the sword.

Under ordinary circumstances, this would increase the distance between the opponent and the blade.

However, the wielder can manipulate the opponent's movement vector so that their attempted retreat instead carries them **forward, toward the sword**.

The opponent still attempts to move backward, but their actual movement is redirected.

This creates a situation in which a defensive action can produce the opposite of its intended result.

The precise extent to which the ability can manipulate speed, rather than direction alone, has not yet been established.

#### Example 1: Turning a Retreat Into an Attack Opportunity

Imagine Salore is holding the Vector-Manipulating Sword and preparing to slash an opponent.

The opponent sees the incoming strike and immediately tries to jump backward to avoid it.

Ordinarily:

1. Salore swings his sword.
2. The opponent jumps backward.
3. The opponent moves out of the blade's reach.
4. The attack misses.

However, Salore can manipulate the opponent's movement vector.

Instead:

1. Salore begins his sword strike.
2. The opponent attempts to jump backward.
3. Salore redirects the movement vector toward himself.
4. The opponent's attempted backward jump carries them toward the blade instead.
5. The opponent moves into the incoming attack rather than escaping it.

**The opponent's attempt to dodge becomes the very action that places them in danger.**

This does not require Salore to physically grab or pull the opponent toward himself. The movement is being redirected through the sword's supernatural field.

#### Example 2: The Opponent Tries to Outsmart the Sword

The sword becomes particularly dangerous when an opponent discovers that it can reverse their movement.

Imagine the opponent has already seen Salore redirect a backward dodge into forward movement.

They reason that the ability must automatically reverse movement.

The opponent then develops what seems like a clever counterstrategy:

"If moving backward makes me move forward, then I'll deliberately move forward. The sword will reverse my movement, and I'll end up moving backward, safely away from the blade."

The opponent attempts to exploit what they believe is a predictable rule.

However, their assumption is incorrect.

**The Vector-Manipulating Sword does not automatically reverse all movement.**

Salore can choose which movements are affected.

Therefore, when the opponent deliberately moves forward, expecting the field to reverse their direction, Salore simply chooses **not to manipulate that movement**.

The result is straightforward:

1. The opponent expects their forward movement to be reversed.
2. They deliberately move toward the sword.
3. Salore leaves their movement vector unchanged.
4. The opponent continues moving forward normally.
5. They run directly into Salore's blade.

The opponent is defeated not because they failed to understand that vector manipulation was occurring, but because they incorrectly assumed the ability was automatic.

They understood one application of the sword and mistook it for an unchanging rule.

#### Example 3: Redirecting a Sideways Dodge

The same basic principle can apply to movement in other directions.

#### Intuitive Control

Despite the mathematical complexity of manipulating vectors, the sword is extremely intuitive to operate.

The wielder does not need to calculate vector components, angles, or mathematical equations consciously.

They can manipulate the affected movement instinctively.

For example, the wielder does not need to calculate the exact mathematical transformation required to redirect an opponent's retreat toward the blade.

They can simply use the weapon's ability to produce the intended movement.

This makes the sword practical during extremely fast combat, where consciously performing lengthy calculations would be inconvenient.

#### Wielder Exemption

The person wielding the sword is exempt from the field's vector manipulation.

Consequently, the wielder does not have to worry about their own movements being redirected by the sword's effect.

This distinction allows the wielder to maneuver normally while manipulating selected opponents.

### 4. Life-Force-Absorbing Ring

A ring that **gradually drains life force** from living beings across a large area (illustratively **about five miles**) and accumulates it as a reserve of usable energy. The effect is not instantaneous. The wearer can spend that reserve to enhance **strength, reflexes, endurance, and other physical performance**. The enhancement is **temporary and consumable**, not permanently or infinitely accumulating strength. Exact range, extraction rate, storage limit, effect on allies or the wearer, and whether prolonged draining kills remain undecided.

### 5. Rotation-Amplifying Naginata

A **naginata** whose next strike becomes more powerful the **more times it has been spun/rotated beforehand**. The amplification is supernatural, not merely the extra momentum a spinning weapon would ordinarily gain. Repeated rotations can therefore produce an exceptionally powerful blow. There is no limit to how powerful it can get if you continue to spin it, but if you stop even for a tiny moment before striking, all the build up power is lost. It is extremely difficult to keep it stored or even spin it for long times.

### 6. Extending Poisonous Whip

A whip that can extend for **miles**, though **not infinitely**, and **poisons** anyone it strikes. Its distinctive feature is that changing the direction of the swing redirects the **newly extending portion**, while the **already extended length stays where it was**. For example, an initial straight section can remain extended forward while a new section branches rightward at the turning point; another change can send the next section upward. Repeated changes form a connected, angular, polyline-like trajectory instead of swinging the whole existing length into the new direction. This permits unexpected approach angles and long-distance attacks.

Salore keeps a **standard Accialite Powered Gun**, it's basically a standard weapon, nothing special.

### 8. Explosive Chain With Two Black Spherical Weights

An enormous chain with a massive **black spherical weight at each end**. The weights look and feel metallic, though their exact substance is unknown. Swinging the chain so the **two weights collide** produces a **massive explosion in the direction of the motion**, accompanied by exceptionally hot **blue fire** that is unusually difficult to extinguish. The weights also inflict devastating blunt impacts individually.

### 9. Venomous Dragon Spike

An **enormous spike broken from a poisonous dragon** that lived centuries ago. A successful puncture delivers severe poison capable of causing **complete paralysis after approximately ten minutes**. The exact progression, other symptoms, lethality, and possibility of treatment or resistance are unspecified. Its danger comes from the retained dragon venom rather than a complex activation mechanism.

### 10. Skyward Escape Whistle

A **small white, skull-shaped whistle**, somewhat like a miniature elephant skull. Blowing it summons a **chain from the sky** that fastens to the user and **rapidly yanks them upward**, creating an immediate vertical escape. Crucially, **it provides no built-in safe descent**: an ordinary person may survive the initial danger only to fall to their death. Highly durable users such as Salore or Francesca may withstand the fall. It can be paired with item 11 to turn the gained altitude into an attack. Final height, ascent duration, and precise appearance are unspecified.

### 11. Crystallizing Dive-Wing Relic

A relic that manifests **white wings**. The faster the user travels **continuously in the same direction** before an impact, the greater the resulting destructive power. A sufficiently fast dive can devastate an enormous area—potentially an entire forest—and transform the **destroyed/affected region into white crystal**. The relic and the Skyward Escape Whistle have unrelated origins but pair naturally: the whistle launches the user high, and the wings enable a destructive descent. Exact speed-to-impact scaling and crystallization boundaries are unspecified.

### 12. Size-Changing Black Mamba

A **living black mamba**, not an inanimate summoned weapon. It has its **own mind**, can follow directions to attack targets, and can **voluntarily enlarge** from an ordinary black mamba's size; it cannot shrink below approximately normal snake size. For instance, it could coil around a leg while small, then expand to **ten times** its size to overpower or swallow an opponent. Its maximum size and the extent of its intelligence and obedience are unestablished.

The snake **lived inside the bag before Salore bought it**. It needs food, which the goose normally produces for it. It can also eat food left in storage, sometimes to Salore's annoyance. Both creatures are independent inhabitants rather than possessions that become inactive between uses.

### 13. Teleportation Ink

**Black ink**, applied with a brush or otherwise painted onto **surfaces or directly into the air**, creates teleportation destinations. One **contiguous connected ink area** is one destination; separate drops or splatters become **distinct points**. Connecting them physically may change that arrangement, but the numbering consequences are undecided. The ink can remain in place until **physically wiped away**, which is easy; it may stay wet for weeks. Supply is **limited but generous**, not established as infinite.

**Individual numbering:** Each user has a **separate destination network**, numbered in that person's **creation order**. Salore's first mark is *Salore's point 1*; the goose's first mark is *the goose's point 1*. Neither changes the other's numbering.

**Selecting a point:** The user communicates its **number with finger or toe gestures**. A small number can be shown directly; a larger number can be built through successive gestures. For example, to select **50**, repeated sequences of five raised fingers can communicate the cumulative number; the system understands the continuation rather than immediately teleporting to point 5. Gesturing **10** directly selects point 10. This may become tedious for extremely large indices even if the user remembers every destination.

**Transport:** The ability can teleport the **user and other objects** between destinations. It does **not guarantee a safe arrival**. If a point is obstructed by rock, teleporting into it may crush a user with insufficient durability; Francesca's physical resilience can alter the outcome. Offensively, a user might put ink in an opponent's mouth and teleport a **boulder** into the marked location, with consequences dependent on durability and circumstances.

**Use:** Salore keeps **few strategic marks**, sometimes for escapes, to make the list easier to manage. The goose maintains its own extensive network throughout its kingdom to move between construction sites. Francesca's extraordinary brainpower could let her track thousands of locations, but selecting a number such as **10,000** by gestures might be slower than simply traveling there physically. 

### 14. Immortal Goose

An **immortal, centuries-old goose wearing a suit and fedora**, already living in the bag before Salore purchased it. It is **independent, extremely intelligent, wise, and perceptive**, not a dumb animal or automatic servant. Its main interests are **construction, cooking, and reading**. It has built the bag's largely empty kingdom over centuries, keeps a library supplied partly by Salore, uses item 18 to landscape its buildings, and uses item 13 to teleport between sites. Its exact immortality mechanics are unspecified.

**Object generation:** The goose can produce **tangible, practical objects** through its stomach and expel them. This looks superficially like regurgitation, but objects are **new, usable, and not digested vomit**. It can create **rocks, tools, bricks, wood, concrete, building materials, water, and fresh five-star-quality prepared meals**, even objects **larger than itself**, such as an elephant. It is **not unrestricted omnipotence**: it cannot simply produce an abstract proof to an unsolved mathematics problem or invent an arbitrary “kills everyone” device on request. The exact criterion separating possible practical objects from impossible requests is still undecided.

**Relationship with owners:** The goose **sometimes** helps Salore find and quickly pass him a weapon during a fight, or produces food, but can refuse if occupied with construction. It **feeds the black mamba**. It occasionally chats with Salore and cares about him somewhat; however, after witnessing centuries of changing owners, Salore's death is **not a life-shattering event**. Protecting its accumulated work matters greatly. Although the bag's owner can request things, the goose has its own priorities.

**Understanding Francesca:** Within **approximately two days** after Francesca takes the bag, the goose becomes the **only individual known to genuinely understand how she thinks**. This is a process, **not mind-reading**. It first observes her and deduces much of her psychology, then identifies unanswered questions. Francesca **cannot cook**, so when she asks it for food or another favor, it may demand an answer to a question in exchange. She answers; it supplies the requested item. Through repeated, targeted exchanges, it fills the remaining gaps and concludes it understands her completely.

**Mutual awareness and silence:** Francesca **knows** the goose has figured her out. She would be willing to kill an ordinary person if their knowledge threatened to expose her actual motivations and damage her carefully maintained image. But the goose **has no desire to expose her**. It recognizes morally disturbing aspects of her behavior and has opinions about them, yet does not care enough to intervene, leave the bag, or risk the centuries of construction work it values. Francesca understands that it is not a practical threat, so she tolerates it.

### 15. Black Flag

Planting this **black flag** in the ground creates a field that **dramatically slows movement** within its area, including the **user's own movement** and **ordinary projectiles**. The slowing is **relative**: someone originally much faster can remain faster than a slower fighter; the effect does not assign everyone one identical speed. Range, duration, degree of slowing, and any effects on thought or perception are undecided.

**Sniper rifle interaction:** The flag's slowing effect **does apply** to the unstoppable bullet, but the bullet **must maintain its original velocity**. These requirements conflict at the **same instant** the bullet enters the field. Reality corrects the contradiction **simultaneously**, so there is **no moment** in which the bullet actually slows and no later re-acceleration. Its observed speed stays constant. This can let the flag slow an opponent while the bullet travels unchanged, but it still does not guarantee a hit. Describing the bullet as simply immune to the flag misses the simultaneous application-and-correction mechanic.

### 16. Distraction Bell

A **small silver bell** that briefly makes **anyone who hears it lose their train of thought**, even someone concentrating intensely. It causes a momentary interruption, **not permanent amnesia** or continuous mind control. A person who genuinely cannot hear it—because of deafness, adequate ear protection, or another obstruction—is unaffected. Its effect **recharges while unused**; repeated rapid ringing produces **diminishing distraction**, so timing matters. 

### 17. Delayed-Voice Megaphone

A **non-electronic megaphone** with **physical delay-setting knobs**. When the user speaks into it, their words are **completely inaudible at the time of speaking**. At the chosen delay—even a precise setting such as **45 minutes, 50 seconds**—the original speech plays **from the exact place where it was spoken**, regardless of where the user or megaphone has moved. It is a timed, location-anchored sound effect, not a prediction device. Maximum delay, recording capacity, and playback volume are undecided.

**Francesca's “Checkmate”:** After Salore dies in the **G-Rank Free For All**, Francesca takes the bag, finds this megaphone during the continuing battle, sets its delay, and silently records **“Checkmate.”** She then fights for several dozen more minutes. At the calculated final moment, she kills the last remaining enemy G-rank combatant **from a distance with the unstoppable sniper rifle**, at the **same location where she originally spoke**. The delayed word plays at precisely that moment, becoming the opponent's final audible declaration.

### 18. Infinite Vegetation Spray Bottle

A spray bottle with an **endless supply of liquid** that transforms **contacted material itself** into **soil/dirt, grass, and flowers**. It does **not** merely grow plants on top of an intact object. To open a locked or extremely durable chest, the user could spray its surface, **shovel away the transformed soil**, and repeat until an opening exists. The same process affects **living flesh**: spraying someone's face could turn affected tissue into dirt and vegetation, causing catastrophic injury.

The **goose normally uses this bottle for gardening**, planting greenery around the cities and buildings inside the bag. Consequently, Salore cannot always access it conveniently; the goose may be busy landscaping with a potentially lethal relic.

### 19. Weightless-Wielder Hammer

An **enormous hammer** with a striking head **roughly the size of half an elephant**. Its weight is so extreme that a hit is compared to being slammed by **multiple blue whales' worth of weight** (illustrative, not a calibrated mass). Yet it feels **effectively weightless to the wielder**, who can swing it freely without bearing that weight. **The exemption applies to wielding, not to being struck**: accidentally hitting oneself can be instantly fatal to an ordinary person. Its exact mass, construction, and behavior after being thrown or released are unspecified.

### 20. Indestructible Black Umbrella

A conventional-looking **black umbrella** that opens and closes normally but is **otherwise extraordinarily indestructible**. It was owned by the ancient **vampire monarch who created the bag**. In his era, **sunlight could burn vampires to death**, so he used it to shade himself while going outdoors. Indestructibility ensured that an opponent could not simply destroy his shade and leave him fatally exposed. **Modern vampires have evolved** and are no longer harmed by sunlight in the same way; the umbrella's original purpose is therefore historically specific.

Its protection does **not override the unstoppable sniper bullet**: if the bullet's path intersects the umbrella, reality creates a passage so the bullet can continue. This is an interaction between absolute movement and apparent indestructibility, not evidence that ordinary attacks can damage the umbrella.

### 21. Love or Die — Bond Bracelet

A supernatural bracelet embodying an extreme romantic commitment. **Two people must mutually agree** to the arrangement; **only one** then wears the bracelet. Once the bond is established, **removing it causes the wearer to die instantly**, regardless of whether the removal reflects a change of heart. **Losing the entire arm** also counts: a bracelet still around a **severed wrist** is no longer attached to its wearer and triggers death, preventing that loophole.

Salore **frequently proposes** using it with Francesca because he **genuinely loves her**, not because he wants her dead. He suggests that **she wear it**, and even offers to **wear it himself**. Francesca **refuses the arrangement**: a fighter like Salore could lose an arm by accident in battle and instantly die. **Neither actually activates or wears it under the agreement.** The mutual-consent procedure and other possible exceptions remain undefined.

### 22. Soul-Attacking Saber

A sword **somewhat shaped like a saber**, visually distinct from a conventional katana, that **directly damages souls**. Against a person possessing a soul, its blade can **pass through the physical body** without leaving an ordinary visible cut while **damaging the soul itself**. That injury **does not manifest as a matching physical wound**. The exact consequences of soul damage, including lethality, healing, and spiritual resistance, remain unspecified.

The weapon is **not universally intangible**. **Soul-less material such as armor can obstruct its blade**; a soul inside armor does not automatically cause the armor to become permeable. **Thin clothing may not prevent contact**, but its precise interaction with clothing is unfinalized. It is conceptually reminiscent of the *Soul Split Katana* from *Jujutsu Kaisen*, without automatically sharing that weapon's full mechanics. The saber's exact design is undecided.

### 23. Lightning Execution

**“Lightning Execution” is Salore's name**, not the item's known original name, for a supernatural **party trick** once intended to impress spectators, possibly including emperors. It makes the **user, their clothing, and objects they hold invisible**, while simultaneously spawning a **separate lightning-like particle or orb** whose movement the user controls. The orb races around **at lightning speed**, leaving a visible trail. To spectators it seems the performer has **transformed into lightning** and can fly around at extreme speed. **The person never transforms or gains that speed**; they remain invisible somewhere else.

**Combat deception:** An opponent may focus on the darting lightning, mistakenly believing it to be the fighter, while the invisible user **walks behind them and attacks with a concealed sword**. This fails against opponents who understand the trick, can detect invisible bodies by other senses or supernatural means, or notice environmental clues such as **grass bending or footprints**. Invisibility does not make the user intangible or erase their physical interactions. The item's physical form, effect duration, orb range, and whether the lightning has any actual electrical or physical attack effect are unspecified.

## Francesca's significance

The bag matters directly to Francesca because she **takes it from Salore's body before the G-Rank Free For All has ended** and uses its weapons against surviving G-rank opponents. Most notably, she combines the **sniper rifle**, **delayed-voice megaphone**, and her own **high-speed local calculations** to time the final opponent's death with the playback of **“Checkmate.”** The megaphone does not predict the future; her brain supplies the calculation. She retains the bag afterward without routinely carrying it into later fights.

The bag also places Francesca in contact with the goose, **the only known individual to understand her genuine psychology**. It reaches that understanding in about **two days**, partly by exchanging food or favors for answers to targeted questions. Francesca knows she has been understood but tolerates the goose because it has **no motivation to expose her** and would rather protect the work of centuries.
