# Vengeful Spirits

There are many vengeful spirits, about a dozen throughout the entire main series, and the sequel series. 

The most common examples are [Demon King Malu](../other-characters/demon-king-malu.md) and [Salore](../other-characters/salore), these are both vengeful spirits. In terms of how they became vengeful spirits, that is explained separately in their own documents.

Vengeful spirits are the dead who refuse to pass.

There is a dimension in the setting of the story's universe, that is the place In Between the Living and the Dead. When you die, your soul travels from the living world to the dead, the place in between is the medium you travel through.

If you die, but you have very strong emotions, for examples, something you must protect, something you absolutley cannot let go, if your will is strong enough, your soul may resist so much, that you are unable to cross the dimension in between. This is extremely rare, and only a dozen in the history of the entire series' settings.

There is actually an entire arc revolving around vengeful spirits, however it is not relevant to Francesca and the context describing her (because this repo is primarily about Francesca if you read it's purpose in the other docs), since its not relevant to Francesca, we haven't' included that arc, even though the arc was large, and has it's own main characters. But it isn't relevant to Francesca so we didn't include that. Other than the vengeful spirit from that arc, the other 2 notable vengeful spirit's are Demon King Malu and Salore.

Vengeful Spirits can only disappear and pass once they accomplish or fulfill their objective. For example, the spirit in the unnamed arc passes when their objective or reason for being unable to pass was resolved. Salore for example, cannot pass as long as Francesca is alive, because his will and objective, is wanting to protect Francesca, so it is impossible for him to pass until Francesca doesn't exist. Demon King Malu's objective is to get revenge on the hero party that defeated him, however that party is long dead, therefore he cannot get revenge, therefore he cannot pass.

Vengeful Spirits must a place to be locked too, if they cannot pass the in between ,they are rejected and sent back to where they came from, but where would they stay? They do not wander around, and must have something grounding their existence. For example, in the vengeful spirit arc, the protagonist of that arc deals with a spirit inhabiting his necklace, a gift he gave to the original person before they became a spirit. Demon King Malu is grounded in the [Black Ring](./black-ring.md). Salore is grounded in his [Weapon Bag](./salores-weapon-bag.md). Importantly, the grounding place does not restrict where the spirit can go, it has no restriction, it just represents where their soul is. Where a spirit can go, do, and etc depends on the individual. Destroying the grounding place may not remove the spirit, it could find another grounding place, or start wandering, that is the only time where a spirit could become a Wandering vengeful Spirit. The only difference with a normal vs wandering spirit is that a wandering has no grounding place, that is the only exception where you do not need a grounding place. Usually if a grounding place is destroyed, the spirit's soul finds and resides in another grounding place nearby. 

The vengeful spirit from the unspecified arc cannot manifest, or become physical, they can supply magical power to the protagonist who has the necklace, the protagonist can attempt to communicate with her (the spirit), but the spirit is not intelligent and cannot respond back intelligently, it responds more with noises. Salore as a vengeful spirit is not intelligent either, and responds in grunts, however he can manifest physically, Francesca manifests him physically and can control him at will like a puppet. The Demon King Malu as a spirit cannot manifest physically either, however he IS intelligent, and can possess people, a trait not seen in the other 2 described spirits. In terms of intelligence, Malu is the only vengeful spirit who is truly intelligent. Really, how a vengeful spirit behaves and can do is dependent on who becomes a vengeful spirit, there is no specific law or rule governing these traits. However the rules about grounding, how vengeful spirits emerge and etc are true for every vengeful spirit.

Spirits are also made out of magic entirely, more precisely their souls are not magic, they are real souls (souls exist in the story's setting), however for the soul to do anything other than being a soul, like manifesting, or something, they need a means / medium to do it. For example, the Malu as a spirit cannot simply possess somebody, a soul has no trait or property with that ability, so the soul's own will is strong enough to use magic, and request / call magic particles from the sphere, using the magic as a medium to possess people. Salore cannot simply manifest physically, a soul has no property allowing that, therefore, with the intense will of the soul alone, it requests / calls magic particles, using magic as a medium to manifest itself. Francesca can also manually manifest Salore due to the nature of this particular spirit. So anything a vengeful spirit does, that is not simply existing as a soul, is magic. So Salore, as a physical manifestation is purely magic, like a magic golem in simple terms. 

Again, spirits are extremely rare and only a dozen in the history of the series exist, so imagine how intense the will has to be.
