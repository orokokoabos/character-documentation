# Ačilite (Achilite)

## Overview

**Accialite** is a crystalline mineral with an exceptionally strong affinity for magical energy.

Its most important property is its ability to absorb and store magic, making it an important material in the development of magical technology and military equipment.

Accialite was first discovered in the **Great Cliffs** by a joint expedition involving **Great Columbia** and **Molgrun**.

Accialite has a distinctive reddish-black coloration.

It naturally occurs in clusters composed of angular crystals with exceptionally sharp edges.

Its appearance can be compared to clusters of jagged glass.

Accialite is extremely hard.

Its resistance to scratching is comparable to that of diamond.

However, high hardness does not mean that Accialite is impossible to break.

The material is also brittle.

A sufficiently powerful blunt impact can fracture its crystalline structure.

Consequently, Accialite should not be described as indestructible simply because of its hardness.

## Magical Properties

Accialite possesses a powerful affinity for magical energy.

It can absorb magic and retain the absorbed energy within its crystalline structure.

Once magical energy has been absorbed, Accialite can store it indefinitely until the crystal is destroyed.

This makes it valuable as a medium for retaining magical energy for later use.

Its ability to store magic is the primary reason it has been incorporated into various forms of magical technology.

The precise mechanics of magical energy absorption and release depend on the broader magic system.

Accialite has been used in several types of military equipment.

Its ability to absorb and store large quantities of magical energy allows it to serve as an energy source for powerful weapons.

### The Sound Cannon

One notable application of Accialite was the development of the **Sound Cannon** during the **Expansion Era (Chimera War)**.

Researchers were able to force the **Wailer (Sound Stone)**, while in its statue form, to leak magical energy.

The researchers captured this energy and stored it in enormous clusters of Accialite.

These magically charged clusters were then used as the foundation of the Sound Cannon.

When fired, the cannon released the stored power of the Wailer in the form of an extraordinarily concentrated, invisible shockwave.

The shockwave could cause devastating damage.

It was capable of tearing through fortifications and destroying eardrums.

Entire groups of soldiers could be deafened, while defensive walls could be destroyed by a single attack.

This demonstrated the military potential of capturing magical energy in Accialite and releasing it through specialized equipment.

Ačilite has been used in many ways, offensively, defensively, or for normal everyday use, although it is extremely expensive. One notable example is how Ačilite is used with guns: [Guns](./guns.md)
