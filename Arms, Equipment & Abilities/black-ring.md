# Black Ring

The Black Ring is an artifacts discovered by the demons centuries in the past before the main events of the story, gifted to the Demon King, and passed down as tradition. If the king dies, it's passed down to the next demon king or leader, not by blood but by who actually leads the demon nation. It has been passed down dozens and dozens of times.

It is said to be the home for all the rulers of the Demon Nation after they die.

It does not necessarily have any special abilties or features, but it is believed to have them. It is mostly a tradition.

Because of the nature of how [Vengeful Spirits](./vengeful-spirits.md) work, the original Demon King Malu is trapped inside ring as a vengeful spirit. He does not really interfere with anything or do anything, however people who wear the black ring, could technically be visited by Malu in their head if he chooses to talk to the wearer, the wearer could also request an audience with Malu. It mostly happens in their head, you can hear him talking to you through your mind. 

Famously, during the final arcs of the main series, Demon King Orca uses the Black Ring to request an audience with Malu, asking him for help in defeating Francesca, so Malu as a vengeful spirit possess Orca's body, and uses it to fight Francesca. However they were mostly in a stalemate, neither could lose or win, and eventually, the vengeful spirit's possession time is up and he is unable to continue in Orca's body, and lost possession, giving Orca's body back, and ending the fight.

While the Black Ring is not really important in the main story and barely talked about, only introduced very late briefly. During the sequel of the story, in the sequel story folder of this repo, the Black Ring plays a key part in the central conflict.
