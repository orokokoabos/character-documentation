## The Main Conflict in the Sequel Story

In the sequel story, it is set many centuries after the events of the main story and Tarlylox is NOT cured, however it is no longer an immediate threat to your life, medication advancements allow infected to live a happy life until old age (old age relative to the type of sub human or human, not all sub humans and humans have the same average life span of course). Before, Tarlylox could take your life in a few years, currently Tarlylox takes your life in a dozen or few dozen years. However it is not cured, and your lifespan is still shortened by it, just not as significantly as before.

The main issue is that there is a dangerous cult, who worship the Demon King Malu. The cult emerged in the past decade before the events of the sequel story, and they have discovered a way to fully resurrect Malu. His resurrection process causes disasters world wide, forests start rotting, water in some areas get sludgy and black, birds randomly fall out of the sky, black ooze could be coming out of random spots on a mountain, the source is unknown, basically very weird things are happening. You could walk into a forest, and find all the animals dead and rotten.

The immediate response by the world is to fix and solve these crisis worldwide, part of the story is about figuring out how they are planning to revive him, how to stop the resurrection, who the cult is and how to defeat them, and etc. The other part is about the damages it causes to the world they have to try and mitigate, like you would have to evacuate a rural village if their forest is getting corrupted, their live stock getting infected and dying, its dangerous to live there, for example. It is a huge world wide environmental issue basically, they also need to figure out why resurrecting the demon king causes all of this weird stuff to happen. 

The environmental oddities emerging world wide is basically a sign telling you "The Original Demon King is waking up". 

Also the demon king is mostly not even part of the story / has barely any screen time, it's mostly about how to world responds and it follows a few protagonists trying to defeat the cult as they continue their progress in resurrecting the demon king Malu. He appears shortly when he finally gets resurrected.
