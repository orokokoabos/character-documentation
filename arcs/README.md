# Arcs

Arcs that Francesca participate in, and what happens.

## How to read the arcs section of the repo?

These do not document all the events of the arcs, it only documents Francesca's part in the arc, and other information if it is directly relevant to Francesca's part. So parts of the arc that Francesca is not relevant in or isn't required for you to understand Francesca's part are ommited because it is not talking about Francesca Badu.

So to not think everything put in any of the arcs .md is the entire arc, it's not, it's only talking about the parts of the arc that Francesca is in.

We also do not document arcs that Francesca is not in (unless it is relevant to her or helps understand her). So that is why we haven't documented 50+ arcs, because 99% of them have nothing to do with Badu, so we didn't document it.

Also in each arc's file, we usually talk about only the parts relevant to Francesca or for context, so even in an arc's own file, 99% of the actual arc's information might not be mentioned, because that's not what we're focusing on. For example, in the TTC arc file, we will only talk about the end of the arc because that is relevant to Francesca, however that skips 99% of the actual arc, and we are not going to talk about or mention the rest of the arc's 99%, because it has nothing to do with Francesca and barely adds any context for Francesca.

## Why Is the Arcs Directory at the Repository Root?

Although the repository primarily exists to document Francesca Badu, certain other characters and events must also be documented to provide the necessary context for understanding her.

These supporting character documents may need to reference events described in the arc documentation.

For this reason, the /arcs directory is located at the repository root rather than inside /francesca-badu.

This organizational choice does not change the repository's primary focus. Arc documentation remains predominantly centered on Francesca Badu, and other characters and events are documented primarily to the extent that they help explain or contextualize her.

# Other Arcs

Sometimes, an arc is documented inside a characters own file [see other characters](../other-characters). the reason for this is, the character's arc may have no importance to Francesca, therefore it is not documented in /arcs (this folder). However the character itself does have importance to Francesca, that is why we document this character, but if we simply document the character, it misses a lot of the "meat" of their story because their arcs are not documented. That is why SOMETIMES, in SOME of the characters, we include their arcs inside their character file or folder. If we include their arc in /arcs (this folder), we risk crowding the folder with arcs and stories that do not relate to Francesca, so instead, we put those arcs/stories inside the character files itself instead of in arcs. So SOME of the missing arcs, may actually be documented in other characters in their own file / folder, but they are not documented in /arcs (this folder). this does not mean EVERY missing arc is documented somewhere in another character, we still omit and havent documented like 99% of all the arcs in the story of the entire series, but we are saying that SOMETIMES there will be arcs thay are not documented in /arcs, but are documented in another character's own folder/file. for example Aidos's character file shows information about arcs he participated in. even though we dont see those arcs in /arcs, we see it in his character documentation. Or another example, Meursault has his own arcs in his character file, but no arcs are documented in /arcs. There are other characters who have arcs documented in their character file/folder too. For any other information on other possible arcs, check the other characters folder. but again, even with those additional arcs, thats only about 1% of the entire story, 99% of the entire story id not documented at all in this repo because it has nothing to do with Francesca.
