# Post–G-Rank Free For All Arc

## Overview

The Post–G-Rank Free For All arc takes place following the conclusion of the G-Rank Free For All.

This arc is significant because it establishes some of the political and ideological consequences of Francesca Badu's actions during the preceding conflict.

It also marks the first introduction of [Leon](../other-characters/leon/README.md), who eventually becomes the principal organizer and leader of the international anti-demi-human coalition / Leon's Regime.

Although this arc concerns political developments extending beyond Francesca herself, those developments are documented because they are directly relevant to understanding her later role and reputation.

## Background: The Existing Political Stalemate

Before the G-Rank Free For All, the relationship between humans and demi-humans is already a major source of international political conflict.

The incurable disease associated with demi-humans creates a genuine public health threat.

Different nations, organizations, and individuals advocate widely different responses, ranging from protection and coexistence to separation and extermination.

Powerful supporters exist on opposing sides of the conflict.

Neither side is easily capable of imposing its preferred policies without risking resistance from other major political and military powers.

The result is a prolonged stalemate.

The ideological conflict already exists before Francesca's actions.

## Francesca's Actions During the Preceding Arc

During the G-Rank Free For All, Francesca fights numerous extraordinarily powerful individuals and kills many of them.

Several of those individuals are demi-humans.

However, Francesca's motivation is unrelated to the political conflict between humans and demi-humans.

She fights powerful opponents because defeating them contributes to the extraordinary reputation she desires.

She does not deliberately organize a political campaign, pursue demi-humans as a group, or attempt to alter the international political balance.

Nevertheless, the deaths of these exceptionally powerful individuals significantly affect the world's balance of power.

Some of the deceased combatants had represented major potential obstacles to military aggression.

Among those killed is an anti-demi-human G-rank individual associated with the World Government's leadership.

This death contributes to a change in the balance of political power within the World Government, producing a pro-demi-human majority.

These developments create new opportunities and concerns for opposing political movements.

## Leon's First Appearance

Leon is introduced in this arc.

Before the events of the G-Rank Free For All, Leon already possesses a strong ideological conviction that demi-humans must be exterminated to prevent humanity's eventual extinction.

He believes the continued spread of their disease will ultimately lead to a catastrophe affecting humanity as a whole.

However, he has previously felt powerless to act because of the extraordinary political and military resistance that such a campaign would provoke.

When Leon learns of Francesca's victories and the deaths of numerous powerful combatants, he recognizes that the situation has changed.

Individuals whose strength previously made a large-scale anti-demi-human campaign extraordinarily dangerous have been eliminated.

For Leon, this development creates an opportunity that did not previously seem available.

He decides to begin actively pursuing his objective.

He approaches different nations, organizations, and influential groups in an attempt to assemble a coordinated anti-demi-human movement.

This process eventually leads to the formation of a major international coalition.

## Francesca Becomes a Symbol

Leon interprets Francesca's actions as extraordinarily significant to the future of humanity.

He regards her as exceptionally powerful, beautiful, and intelligent.

He also comes to view her as a savior whose actions have made humanity's supposed liberation possible.

Leon begins elevating her as the symbolic figurehead of his movement.

However, his interpretation of Francesca's actions does not accurately reflect her motivations.

Francesca did not kill her opponents in order to assist Leon or advance an extermination campaign.

She was pursuing her own reputation.

Despite this, the growing admiration directed toward her is something she personally enjoys.

Her willingness to accept recognition from Leon's movement does not establish that she shares its ideology.

Leon is the individual primarily responsible for organizing the movement, while Francesca's importance is largely symbolic.

## Consequences for Francesca's Later Role

Francesca's actions during the G-Rank Free For All unintentionally contribute to the breakdown of the existing political stalemate.

Her victories change the perceived military balance and help create the circumstances in which Leon begins organizing his coalition.

As the ideological conflict escalates, Francesca becomes increasingly associated with the anti-demi-human movement despite her personal indifference toward its objectives.

Her reputation and extraordinary combat capabilities make her an important symbol for Leon's supporters and a major perceived threat to the opposing coalition.

These developments contribute to the circumstances that eventually place her at the center of the final conflict.

Leon's Inspiration and the Formation of the Anti-Demi-Human Movement

Before the G-Rank Free For All, Leon genuinely believes that humanity is heading toward eventual extinction because of the disease transmitted by demi-humans.

He believes exterminating demi-humans is necessary to prevent this outcome. However, despite his strong convictions, he is unable to meaningfully act on them.

The international political situation is effectively a stalemate. Powerful nations, organizations, and individuals support demi-humans, while others oppose them. Any attempt to initiate a large-scale extermination campaign risks provoking overwhelming military resistance and potentially catastrophic war.

Leon understands these obstacles.

He experiences considerable despair and helplessness because he believes humanity is approaching extinction while he lacks the power to prevent it.

This changes when he hears the news that Francesca has defeated and killed numerous extraordinarily powerful individuals, including several demi-humans. Some of those individuals would otherwise have represented major obstacles to anyone attempting to initiate a worldwide anti-demi-human campaign.

Upon learning of these events, Leon realizes that the existing balance of power has been disrupted.

For the first time, he believes that the movement he has wanted to establish might actually succeed.

The news becomes a defining turning point for him. His previous despair and helplessness give way to determination.

Leon interprets Francesca's victories as an extraordinary opportunity for humanity. He begins to regard her as a savior whose actions have made it possible to pursue what he considers humanity's necessary liberation.

However, Francesca has not acted on Leon's behalf. She is unaware of, or indifferent to, the ideological significance he assigns to her victories. Her objective during the G-Rank Free For All was to improve her own reputation by defeating powerful opponents.

Nevertheless, her actions inspire Leon to begin pursuing his ideology on an international scale.

Rather than remaining inactive, Leon begins traveling, approaching, and communicating with individuals, organizations, political groups, and nations around the world.

He seeks out groups that already oppose demi-humans and attempts to persuade them to cooperate under a common movement.

These groups do not necessarily share identical motivations or beliefs. Some genuinely regard demi-humans as an existential threat because of the disease. Others are motivated by political interests, hostility, prejudice, or different proposed responses to the perceived danger.

Leon works to bring these otherwise separate groups together despite their differences.

He uses the changed geopolitical situation, the perceived opportunity for military success, and Francesca's extraordinary reputation to encourage cooperation.

What begins as Leon gathering supporters gradually develops into an increasingly organized international political and military movement.

Through his recruitment, organization, and consolidation of different factions, this movement eventually becomes the anti-demi-human coalition, also associated with Leon's regime.

Leon emerges as its principal ideological and organizational leader.

Francesca, meanwhile, is elevated as the movement's symbolic savior. Leon and his followers attribute extraordinary ideological significance to her victories, even though she does not personally share their objectives.

Francesca's actions create the opportunity, but Leon is the individual who recognizes that opportunity and actively transforms it into an organized international movement.

The formation of this coalition becomes one of the major consequences of the G-Rank Free For All and contributes directly to the eventual outbreak of the worldwide ideological war.

## Scope of This Documentation

This document does not describe every political, military, or personal event occurring during the Post–G-Rank Free For All arc.

It focuses on the consequences of Francesca's earlier actions, Leon's introduction, and the developments necessary to understand Francesca's later significance.

The political movements and international conflict have causes extending far beyond Francesca herself.

**Francesca is an unintended catalyst for the escalation, not the original cause of the underlying ideological conflict.**
