# Identity and Reputation Management

## Overview

Francesca Badu places extraordinary importance on her physical appearance, public image, reputation, and how other people perceive her.

She desires to be recognized as an exceptionally beautiful, powerful, accomplished, and humble individual.

Her concern is not simply obtaining fame or receiving attention. She cares about the specific impression she creates and the characteristics other people associate with her.

Importantly, Francesca's desired public image remains largely consistent across her different identities.

During her service in the Grand Navy (around teenage Francesca - Adult Francesca), she wants to be recognized as an extraordinarily powerful, beautiful, and humble Navy Captain whom criminals cannot defeat.

After abandoning her original identity and becoming Pheme, she wants to be recognized as an extraordinarily powerful, beautiful, and humble criminal whom the authorities cannot defeat.

The fundamental qualities she wants people to recognize remain similar.

What changes is the identity through which she pursues that recognition and the activities associated with maintaining it.

This change does not represent a fundamental reversal of her moral values, emotional disposition, or personality.

## 1. Reputation as a Personal Priority

Francesca intensely values how she is perceived by others.

She wants people to recognize her extraordinary capabilities, accomplishments, attractiveness, and humility.

However, she does not necessarily want to obtain this recognition through explicit boasting, aggressive self-promotion, or openly demanding admiration.

She also cares about appearing genuinely humble and unassuming.

Consequently, maximizing the number of people who know about her is not necessarily more important than preserving the particular impression she wants those people to have.

She may prefer to receive less recognition if obtaining additional recognition would require behaving in a way that contradicts her desired image.

For Francesca, reputation is not simply a matter of popularity. It concerns the qualities associated with her identity and the way others perceive those qualities.

Her desire to appear humble is particularly important and is discussed further in [Social Behavior and Strategic Humility](./social-behavior-and-strategic-humility.md).

## 2. Teenage Francesca to Adult Francesca — Her Grand Navy Identity

During her teenage years, Francesca serves as a Captain in the Grand Navy.

Her position provides a social identity associated with maintaining order, opposing criminals, protecting civilians, and upholding the law.

Francesca seeks to cultivate an extraordinary reputation within this identity.

She wants people to regard her as an exceptionally capable Navy Captain, someone whose power makes her nearly impossible for criminals to defeat.

She also wants to be perceived as exceptionally beautiful, humble, pleasant, and admirable.

Her desire to cultivate this reputation strongly influences her behavior.

Because her identity is associated with law enforcement and public protection, she generally pursues accomplishments that reinforce those associations.

Defeating criminals, protecting people, completing missions, and performing actions regarded as heroic can all contribute to the reputation she wants.

However, this does not mean she possesses an intrinsic commitment to justice, compassion, or moral goodness.

She may perform genuinely beneficial actions without being motivated by concern for the people who benefit.

Her motivations are primarily connected to her personal desires, particularly her reputation and public image.

This distinction is important because Francesca's externally prosocial behavior does not necessarily reflect an internally prosocial moral orientation.

She is not inherently pursuing goodness for its own sake.

She is pursuing the identity and reputation she desires, and within the Grand Navy, that generally encourages behavior regarded as good.

### Limits of Her Grand Navy Persona

Francesca's reputation-oriented behavior does not guarantee that every action she takes is morally good.

She is capable of committing extreme acts of cruelty when doing so serves something she personally values.

Her emotional indifference toward suffering remains present during this period.

Her Grand Navy identity influences her behavior, but it does not fundamentally alter her underlying emotional priorities.

Further explanation of her emotional indifference can be found in [The Ant Analogy](./the-ant-analogy.md).

## 3. Post Admiral Francesca - Abandoning Her Original Identity

Following the events surrounding her dismissal from the Grand Navy, Francesca abandons the identity she previously cultivated.

Her dismissal damages the reputation associated with her original identity.

Rather than continuing to operate under an identity whose public perception has been damaged, she chooses to establish a different one.

She performs surgery on herself (with basically no experience except for her intelligence, motor skills and tools and studying from books for a short amount of time since her dismissal), and adopts a different identity. She does not have a name yet but eventually as people learn about her (not knowing it's Francesca), they started calling her Pheme so that name just stuck, her new identity became Pheme.

This decision should not automatically be interpreted as shame, self-hatred, or an inability to accept her past.

She is not necessarily attempting to erase her past because she cannot psychologically tolerate it.

Instead, she chooses to discontinue her previous public identity and establish a new one.

The details of the events leading to her dismissal belong in the relevant arc documentation.

The psychological significance is that Francesca is willing to abandon a long-established identity when she no longer wishes to maintain the reputation associated with it.

This does not mean that her underlying desires have disappeared.

Rather, she adopts a new identity through which she can continue pursuing them.

## 4. Pheme — Her Criminal Identity

After leaving the Grand Navy, she begins cultivating a reputation as an extraordinary criminal.

Her desired reputation remains similar in several important respects.

She still wants to be recognized as exceptionally beautiful, powerful, accomplished, and humble.

However, instead of wanting criminals to regard her as an unbeatable Navy Captain, she now wants people to regard her as an extraordinarily powerful criminal whom the authorities cannot defeat.

She seeks criminal notoriety and recognition.

Her criminal identity provides a different framework through which she can establish an extraordinary public reputation.

Unlike her previous position in the Grand Navy, this identity is not dependent upon maintaining the public reputation of a law-enforcement officer.

Consequently, accomplishments associated with criminality can now contribute to the identity she wants to cultivate.

This can motivate her to commit serious crimes.

However, her criminal behavior does not necessarily arise from an intrinsic desire to cause suffering, spread evil, or violate moral principles.

The criminal accomplishments are valuable to her because of their relationship to her identity and reputation.

She does not need to enjoy the suffering caused by a crime in order to consider committing it worthwhile.

### Infamy Does Not Necessarily Mean Wanting to Be Hated

Pheme does not necessarily want ordinary people to personally dislike her.

She wants recognition of her extraordinary criminal power, capabilities, accomplishments, and status.

She may want people to regard her as an intimidatingly powerful criminal while simultaneously finding her beautiful, humble, pleasant, and personally likable.

These desires are compatible.

Being notorious for criminal accomplishments does not require her to behave rudely, aggressively, or unpleasantly in ordinary social interactions.

Her desired criminal reputation concerns her capabilities and accomplishments, not necessarily a desire to appear personally cruel or hateful.

## 5. Continuity Between Francesca and Pheme

The transition from Francesca to Pheme does not fundamentally change her core psychological motivations.

Before and after the transition, she continues to place extraordinary importance on physical appearance, reputation, recognition, and her desired public image.

Her preference for appearing humble also remains.

She does not suddenly develop an intrinsic desire to cause suffering after becoming a criminal.

Likewise, her earlier service in the Grand Navy did not mean she intrinsically desired to relieve suffering.

Her emotional indifference toward others' welfare remains consistent with the psychology described in the other documentation.

What changes is the social identity she chooses to cultivate and the kinds of accomplishments that reinforce that identity.

As a Navy Captain, she generally benefits from performing actions associated with heroism and law enforcement.

As Pheme, she benefits from accomplishments associated with criminal notoriety and extraordinary power.

**Her underlying motivation remains centered on her personal desires rather than on goodness or evil as intrinsic goals.**

## 6. Identity Does Not Completely Determine Behavior

Although her chosen identity strongly influences which accomplishments she pursues, it does not determine every action she takes.

Francesca can commit terrible acts while serving in the Grand Navy.

Pheme can behave kindly and perform beneficial actions despite being a criminal.

This is not inherently contradictory.

Her actions are ultimately influenced by her personal priorities, interests, circumstances, and the consequences she anticipates.

Neither identity creates an absolute obligation in her mind to perform only good or only evil actions.

Her social behavior may remain remarkably consistent across both identities, particularly her humility, politeness, and interpersonal pleasantness.

Further explanation is provided in [Social Behavior and Strategic Humility](./social-behavior-and-strategic-humility.md).

## 7. Central Psychological Distinction

Francesca's transition from Grand Navy Captain to criminal does not represent a conventional moral transformation from good to evil.

Her fundamental motivations remain largely consistent.

The distinction is that she changes the identity through which she pursues recognition.

In the Grand Navy, she wants to be recognized as an extraordinarily powerful, beautiful, and humble Navy Captain.

As Pheme, she wants to be recognized as an extraordinarily powerful, beautiful, and humble criminal.

She changes sides without necessarily changing the underlying psychological reasons she wants to be extraordinary.

**Her commitment is primarily to cultivating the identity and reputation she desires, rather than to the moral principles conventionally associated with that identity.**
