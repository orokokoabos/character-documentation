# Authorial and Narrative Context

## Audience Perception and Psychological Concealment

Francesca's true psychological motivations are not fully disclosed through the main story.

The audience primarily observes her outward behavior, dialogue, accomplishments, relationships, and decisions.

Much of her internal psychology is intentionally concealed or left unexplained.

Consequently, the audience may interpret her personality in ways that differ substantially from the underlying psychology established by the authors.

For example, her humility may be interpreted as evidence that she possesses little personal vanity. Her heroic accomplishments may be interpreted as evidence of compassion. Her pleasant interpersonal behavior may be interpreted as evidence of genuine concern for other people's welfare.

These interpretations can be incorrect even when the audience has accurately observed her behavior.

The audience is not expected to reconstruct her entire psychology from the main story.

This is an intentional feature of her characterization, not a requirement that every aspect of her private thinking must be made obvious before the supplementary documentation is read.

## Retrospective Interpretation

The psychological documentation is primarily intended for readers who have already experienced the story.

Its purpose is to provide additional information that allows readers to reconsider what they previously observed.

Ideally, readers may discover that actions they interpreted as expressions of humility, compassion, or moral virtue were motivated by very different psychological priorities.

This does not necessarily mean the earlier behavior was fake.

Francesca can genuinely be pleasant, genuinely experience affection, and genuinely accomplish beneficial things while lacking the motives other people attribute to her.

The distinction is between the behavior itself and the reasons underlying it.

Her psychology can therefore be substantially misunderstood both by people within the fictional world and by the audience observing the story.

## Distinction Between Documentation and Storytelling

The repository is not itself the narrative presentation of the series.

The documentation may explain motivations, intentions, mental processes, and psychological facts that are never explicitly communicated to the audience within the story.
