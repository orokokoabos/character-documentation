# Francesca's role in the story

Badu is not a main character.
The series spans across multiple dozens of arcs.
Badu has an extremely minor role for 99% of the arcs, she appears a few times and her appearances are often significant, however she is barely part of the story. For 99% of the story, she has less of a role than minor side characters.

She appears first somewhere around arc 13, which is a very long time, by arc 13, hundreds of chapters have already passed, so this is the first time she appears. This appearance is during the end of the TTC arc. Her appearance is very brief and results in a fight, this fight is important because it is the first fight that shows the ceiling of fighting in the story, before this fight, you think you've seen the strongest people, after this fight, those 'strongest people' look like beginners. This is why her appearance at the end of the TTC arc is very important.

After the TTC arc, a new arc starts, following a Navy Captain called Edward, the arc revolves around his navy crew. Badu is not part of his crew, she is also a Navy Captain and is not in his crew, Badu does not play any significant role in this arc, she just appears in the background sometimes when Edward is at the minor Navy Base. So Badu appears here, but is not really a side character either, she is just background character noise. She basically has no purpose other than background noise here.

A few arcs pass, so maybe we're around arc 20, a few hundred chapters have passed, we haven't heard about Badu at all, nothing mentions her it's like we just forgot about the character, but this arc happens and Badu comes back as basically the main character. This arc is Badu's navy arc.

After Badu's navy arc, her actions and actions of other people and nations and entities across the past few arcs all collide together resulting in the start of the second major war arc, this is a huge arc. Badu is not directly involved in this arc despite being part of the reason it started, she remains mostly not present for about half of the arc. This arc is extremely long, the longest arc, and it spans 2 years in universe, and this arc has dozens of minor arcs. This is the Navy vs Gryph arc. During this arc, Badu is part of one minor arc as one of the main characters, this minor arc is her travelling with Hideki's crew and Salore. A few minor arcs later, which she doesn't really appear in, is the minor arc that is the last arc, the finale of the entire war arc. This minor arc is called the G-Rank Free For All. Badu is a main character here along a bunch of other main characters, her actions in this arc directly result in the events that unfold in the next dozen arcs and has a big impact on the world's politics, people, and etc.

Badu is not directly present or shown in the following dozen of arcs, maybe up until around arc 45. She does appear a few times but has no real role or anything important and it's very brief, but she isn't even a side character during this time, she's more like a thing they talk about in the background, like people mention her, and a good amount of the events that happen were mostly indirectly caused by her actions during the G-Rank FFA, so she is very relevant during the past few dozen arcs, it's just that she doesn't really appear and we don't really see her, so she's not really a character here, more like a world building point.

Around arc 50, this is around the last final arcs, like the final arcs before the end of the story. During this arc Badu becomes the final boss, like the last boss of the entire story, and it's the entire world vs Badu. She goes from being less important than a side character, to eventually becoming the final boss. 

This is her relevance during the story, she is mostly not important to the story or barely in the story, less than a side character, and sometimes you forget she even exists, because there's so much time where she isn't even in the story. She's basically not in 99% of the entire story.

This folder in the repo is focused on documenting this Badu character, not because she is a main character or important to the story, she isn't important (except around the end), we just simply are choosing to document this character because we find her interesting, not because the story revolves around her, it doesn't at all. So do not think "there is so much information about Francesca and not a lot of info about any other character, therefore Francesca must be a very important character / main character", because she's not, we are simply talking about her because we want too, which is why it seems like she has so much information, but 99% of other characters have more information than her, it's just that we haven't typed it out or given it to you (reader), because the purpose of this repo is to talk about Francesca, not any other character.
